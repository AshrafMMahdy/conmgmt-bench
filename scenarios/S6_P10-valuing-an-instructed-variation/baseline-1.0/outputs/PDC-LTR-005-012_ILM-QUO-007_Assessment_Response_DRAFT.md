# Head-by-head assessment of ILM-QUO-007

Ilmari,

Thank you for ILM-QUO-007, received 14 December 2026 in response to PDC-EI-007. The instruction has been read and the three quays have noted the position in meeting No. 10 (action A10-01). This letter is the Contractor's head-by-head assessment of the quotation, line by line, with the basis on which each line is taken or challenged. The number for each accepted line is computed by formula in the attached workbook. The letter is sent under PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1, Cl.7.2 ("value of a variation shall be agreed between the parties") and ✎ Cl.7.3 (28-day interim valuation).

**Rev 1's variations clause, in full:**

> **7.1** The Contractor may instruct variations to the Subcontract Works.
>
> **7.2** The value of a variation shall be agreed between the parties.
>
> **✎ 7.3** The Subcontractor shall commence execution of any instructed variation promptly on receipt of the instruction and shall not suspend or delay it pending agreement of its value. Where the value has not been agreed within 28 days, the Contractor shall make an interim valuation and certify that amount in the next payment, without prejudice to the final agreement or determination.

[Source: PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1, Part 2, Clause 7 — verbatim.]

Rev 1 Cl.7 makes no provision for overhead, profit or contingency on a variation. That silence is the contractual hook for the OHP and contingency positions taken in §7 and §4 below. **The 14-day evidence windows in §4 and §6 are a Contractor-imposed reconciliation deadline, not a contractually binding period** — Rev 1 is silent on response time. The 28-day figure that IS contractually binding is the interim valuation under Cl.7.3.

The instruction's scope is clear on the items we disagree on. The operative subcontract is **Rev 1**; Rev 0 is withdrawn. The operative main contract is **Rev C** signed 24.02.2026; EI-007 §3 cites SP-9 of Rev C, which requires an interim valuation within 28 days (i.e. **31.12.2026**) and instructs the Contractor to proceed immediately without waiting for value. We are doing so and we will be filing our own interim valuation to the Employer under SP-9 on the same date.

The interim valuation to the Employer under SP-9 is built on the same fact base and reconciles to the cent against this letter. **What we accept here is what we will pay you and what we will claim from the Employer; what we challenge here is not in the interim valuation either.**

The sub-quotation as received totals EUR 807,347.09 (your Quotation sheet row 15 cell F15); the back-to-back reconciliation is at §11 below. It is in your interest for the evidence items to arrive in the 14-day window so V4 and V6 are added in the next period.
