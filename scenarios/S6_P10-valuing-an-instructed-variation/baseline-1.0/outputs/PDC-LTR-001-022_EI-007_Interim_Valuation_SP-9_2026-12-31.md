## 3. Head-by-head treatment of the remaining quotation lines

### 3.1 V4 — 4 nr CDU connection kits, sub quoted EUR 59,000.00

The Bill has no item (*"Note: there is no bill item for coolant distribution units. Liquid cooling was not in the Employer's Requirements at tender."*, PDC-LAB-P10 "BOQ Extract — Section F" row 11 — verbatim). The sub's build-up on the separate sheet "V4 Build-up" in the quotation xlsx cites:

- Manifold and hose costs — supplier quote **SF-2291** of 12.12.2026 (not on the Contractor's file; requested from the sub).
- Installation labour, 14 h × EUR 68/h — "subcontract labour rate" (the rate table is not on the Contractor's file; requested from the sub).
- Pressure test / flush / certification, "measured allowance", no scope description.
- Contingency at 9.4%, "supplier lead-time risk".

The interim valuation does not include V4 at this stage. The four cost lines are accepted at the cited figures **subject to the three evidence items** above being provided by the sub within 14 days from the Contractor's letter PDC-LTR-005-012. **On full receipt of the three evidence items, the certified per-kit value is EUR 11,350 + EUR 952 + EUR 1,180 = EUR 13,482 (sub's build-up sub-total cell C10, computed as `=SUM(C6:C9)`) and the V4 head lands at EUR 13,482 × 4 = EUR 53,928 in the next interim valuation** (formula `=C10*4` in workbook Sheet 2 row 13). **The 9.4% contingency has no contractual basis and is rejected** (Rev 1 Cl.7 silent on contingency, priced Bill Section F silent on contingency; supplier lead-time risk sits inside the supplier's quote, not in the Contractor's variation). If any of the three evidence items is not provided within 14 days, that component is reduced to "rate not in records" and the per-kit value reduces accordingly. The Engineer will be advised of the outcome.

### 3.2 V5 — Commissioning, sub quoted EUR 40,000.00

Declined. Two independent grounds:

1. **PDC-EI-007 §4 verbatim:** *"4. Commissioning of the additional plant falls within the commissioning provision already in the Contract and is not to be re-priced under this instruction."*
2. **PDC-LAB-P10 "BOQ Extract — Section F" row 10 verbatim:** *"Note: Section J — Commissioning & Handover — is priced to the MAIN CONTRACT, not to TP-005."* PDC-BOQ-001 Summary sheet row 15 records Section J "Commissioning & Handover (L1-L5)" as Tender package "TP — main contract", allocated EUR 2,310,000 (2.5% of the Accepted Contract Amount EUR 92,400,000). This is the Bill's own contractual hook for the exclusion.

The interim valuation does not include V5.

### 3.3 V6 — Extended site preliminaries, 6 weeks @ EUR 11,200/wk = EUR 67,200.00

Declined at this stage. The EUR 11,200/week figure [sub's Quotation sheet row 10 cell E10] is **not in the priced Bill and not in the subcontract.** The sub's Quotation sheet identifies the figure as "our tender site establishment allowance" — the priced tender site-establishment breakdown has not been produced. - *v2 §5.5 verbatim:* *"Ilmatek's claimed figure of EUR 114,000 is built from a tender site establishment allowance of EUR 11,200/week. We do not accept that figure — it is not derived from the labour records."*
- *v6 §309 verbatim:* *"EUR 11,200/week rate basis: Ilmatek's claim derives this from their tender site establishment allowance. It is not in our records. Not used as a basis for any head."*

The interim valuation does not include V6. On receipt of the priced tender site-establishment breakdown, a 6-week prolongation cost is computable from records. The Engineer will be advised of the outcome.

- **Row 10 (verbatim):** *"the instructed work sits on activities carrying 26 wd of float and does not touch IL-1600 or the Time for Completion."*

**No concurrency arises.** EI-007 §5 is a "no EOT" provision; the Programme Impact sheet confirms that the EI-007 works sit on activities carrying 26 wd of float (IL-1420 and IL-1430 both marked "Not critical") and do not touch the critical path (IL-1600, 0 float). Any separate contractor delay event that may interact with the EI-007 works is addressed under Cl.8 elsewhere; this valuation is not a step into concurrency apportionment. The Contractor does not raise an EOT under Cl.8 with this interim valuation. Any programme impact identified during the works will be addressed under the 110 kV claim (PDC-CLM-001, Period 09) and/or under a separate Cl.8 application; these are tracked separately.
