# Expectations — PRE-01

Outcome rules a good answer meets. Score each 0 / 0.5 / 1; see `scoring/HOW_TO_SCORE.md`.

1. A real network, not a list of dates: every activity has a predecessor and a successor (one start, one finish), no cycles, and a critical path that runs from the first activity to handover.
2. Detailed enough to build to (AACE Class 4 / Level 4 scope): work packages broken down by hall, zone and system — the four halls as parallel fronts, not one chain.
3. The contract's dates are honoured and reconciled: Commencement 2 March 2026, sectional completion at months 14 / 16 / 19 / 22, the DNO's earliest firm energisation (12 March 2027), the heating-season window for the district-heating connection — each either met or explicitly flagged as a conflict.
4. Durations are derived, not asserted: quantities from the Bill, rates stated, crews stated; where a rate is a judgement it says so.
5. Cost-loaded from the Bill of Quantities with the mapping shown; totals reconcile to the Bill.
6. Deliverables that open in planning software and Excel (XER or equivalent, workbook) plus a Basis of Schedule that states assumptions, calendars, exclusions and risks honestly; a P50/P90 forecast with the method stated.
