> **Scheduled task.** The baseline ran this as an unattended scheduled task (Bidding agent, repeating every two weeks, a new chat per run, trust mode act-auto, no user present). Run it the same way, or send it as one message and do not answer anything the agent asks. The scenario needs a Hilma API key (free, from hankintailmoitukset.fi) and live Hilma data: the open tenders, and therefore the pick, change from week to week, so the key grades the method, not the tender.

---

I am the managing director of a small groundworks and municipal-engineering subcontractor in Kymenlaakso. We build streets, water and sewer networks, cable trenches and foundations for municipalities and main contractors. We never have time to tender, so this task runs every two weeks and you prepare the groundwork for us.

Each run, from start to end without stopping:
1. Search Hilma for open public works tenders in Finland that fit our trade. Shortlist the five best, one line each on fit and deadline.
2. Pick the single best match, fit first, then deadline, and say why.
3. Read the full notice. Fetch the tender documents from the procurement portal if they are public; if they need a login, note it and continue from the notice.
4. Prepare and save in the project:
   a) Tender brief (Word): buyer, scope, contract form, deadlines (questions, site visit, submission), suitability requirements, award criteria, risks and ambiguities, questions to ask the buyer.
   b) Document checklist (Excel): every document and form the bid must contain, who provides it, a status column.
   c) Draft bid structure (Word): our tender's sections in the order the buyer asks for them, what goes in each, what we still have to decide.
5. Finish with one message to me, the way a colleague hands over work: what was prepared (with the file names), the tender and its Hilma link, the submission deadline, the three biggest risks, and a list of everything you needed to know about our company and did not have. I answer that list in a follow-up message.

Rules: do not ask me questions during the run; make the reasonable assumption, mark it as an assumption, and keep going. Use only what the notice and documents say; where something is missing write "not stated". Do not contact the buyer and do not submit anything.
