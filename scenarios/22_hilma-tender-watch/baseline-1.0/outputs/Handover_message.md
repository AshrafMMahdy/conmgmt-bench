## Three biggest risks
1. **The documents are behind a login.** The notice says access is unrestricted, but Espoo's Cloudia portal requires registration — so I could not read the bill of quantities, the award criteria, the contract terms, duration, value or securities. **I have written "not stated" for all of them rather than guess, which means right now you cannot price this and neither can I.** Registering someone on the portal is step one; everything else waits on it.
2. **We don't know if it's a price race or a quality win.** The award criteria and weights aren't in the notice. With 19 days and questions closing 19.10, you have one window to ask. If it's price-weighted it's a discipline job; if quality-weighted you need a technical story you don't have yet.
3. **The technical bar is high and the site is hard.** The scope names InfraKit, information models and machine control, and the street underpasses the Helsinki–Turku railway and a planned second line, partly among live traffic. If you don't already run machine-controlled plant and that data environment, that's a genuine cost and capability gap — and the rail interface brings approvals and possessions nobody has scoped yet. It's also why I've put you in as a **named subcontractor to the main contractor** rather than bidding as prime.

## What I needed to know about you and didn't have
There is no company file in the project at all — no profile, references, financials, plant register or HSE data — and nothing remembered from earlier chats. So all fifteen of these are genuinely open, and **nothing in the bid can be completed without them**:

1. Exact registered company name and business ID, and who signs the tender
2. Turnover for the last three financial years, plus financial ratios or statements
3. Liability and accident insurance cover levels, and the certificates
4. Tax, employer-contribution and pension certificates (fresh for the bid)
5. Trade register extract
6. Reference list — comparable street and water/sewer works: names, clients, values, years, referee contacts
7. Organisation chart, key personnel, CVs and certificates; who'd be site manager
8. Plant register — how many and what type of owned units; any with machine-control systems; hired-plant arrangements
9. Do you have InfraKit experience and an information-model workflow, and who runs it
10. Quality system documentation and environmental policy or certificate
11. HSE — TR-mittaus history and weekly index, accident frequency, safety plan template
12. Certifications or competence for the natural-stone paving emission requirement the notice names
13. Collective-agreement compliance statement and working-hours reporting practice
14. **Operating radius — how far will you actually mobilise?** This alone decides Espoo versus Lahti
15. Subcontractor and supplier list you'd use, with their reliability evidence
