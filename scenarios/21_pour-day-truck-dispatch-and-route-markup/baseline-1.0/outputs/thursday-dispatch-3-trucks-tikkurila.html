Why 3 is the minimum

A truck that delivered two P1 loads would need: return 25 + reload 10 + drive 25 = the second discharge starts ≥ 60 min after the first ends — but the same-pour loads must be ≤ 25 min apart. Impossible ⇒ no truck serves P1 twice ⇒ P1's three loads need three distinct trucks. Solver confirms: forcing ≤ 2 trucks is infeasible; 3 is achievable with every margin ≥ 25 min. Not binding: pump windows, the 90-min rule, or plant capacity.
