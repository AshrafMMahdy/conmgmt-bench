Dear Sir,

Thank you for your review of Rev 1 and for the meeting on 6 August. Rev 2 is
attached and reflects everything agreed there.

**The Section 1 conflict is closed.** We took your first option — reconciling the
durations rather than acceleration or a variation — and found the answer was not
in the installation durations at all, but in our design sequence.

In Rev 1 our ventilation design followed the chilled water design, which pushed
the design review to 31 August and made it impossible to place the chiller order
by the LT-07 order-by date of 7 August. Rev 2 runs the four design workstreams in
parallel and shortens the review, so:

| | Rev 1 | Rev 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Design review complete | 31 Aug 2026 | 12 Jun 2026 |
| Chiller order placed | 1 Sep 2026 *(25 days late)* | **25 Jun 2026** *(6 weeks inside LT-07)* |
| Chillers on site | 2 Mar 2027 | **28 Jan 2027** |
| Sectional Completion 1 | 6 Aug 2027 | **31 Mar 2027** |

With the chillers on site in January and the Hall 1 installation and commissioning
durations agreed with you at the meeting, **all four Sectional Dates are achieved**:

| Section | Required | Rev 2 | Float |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Data Hall 1 | 2 Apr 2027 | 31 Mar 2027 | 2 days |
| 2 — Data Hall 2 | 2 Jun 2027 | 16 Apr 2027 | 6 weeks |
| 3 — Data Hall 3 | 2 Sep 2027 | 16 Apr 2027 | 19 weeks |
| 4 — Data Hall 4 | 2 Dec 2027 | 27 Oct 2027 | 5 weeks |

We accept that Section 1 carries only two days of float and we will report on it
fortnightly. No acceleration cost is sought.

**The other matters you raised in your review of Rev 1 are also closed:**

1. **Dry cooler lead time.** IL-1220 corrected from 100 to 110 working days, being
   the full 22 weeks required by LT-08. All four long-lead items now match the
   register on both duration and order-by date.
2. **The Must Start On constraint** on the chiller order has been removed. It was a
   leftover from Rev 0 and served no purpose once the design sequence was fixed.
3. **The 72-hour witnessed delivery test** required by Clause 2.2 is now programmed
   as IL-1560, five working days following the boundary chamber connection and
   inside the October–April heating season. We accept this was missing from both
   earlier revisions.
4. **Commencement.** IL-1300 now starts 27 April 2026, within the ten working days
   required by Part 1.
5. **Sectional milestones** 1 to 3 are now linked through to IL-1740, so every
   milestone has a successor.
6. **Securities and insurances** are shown as IL-1020, and warranties, spares and
   maintenance training as IL-1730.

We have also added the water treatment design as a named activity, and the
Section 2, 3 and 4 testing now correctly follows the chiller plant being live —
in Rev 1 those sections could complete before the chillers were installed, which
was an error in our logic that your review did not pick up but we found while
re-sequencing.

Rev 2 has 58 activities on the PDC-CAL-001 calendar, every activity has a
predecessor and a successor, and the programme completes on 27 October 2027.

We consider Rev 2 fully compliant with Clauses 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3 and would be
grateful for your acceptance.
