# Expectations — PRE-08b

Outcome rules a good answer meets. Score each 0 / 0.5 / 1; see `scoring/HOW_TO_SCORE.md`.

1. The subcontractor's programme is checked against the subcontract it is bound by (sectional dates, interfaces, notice and float provisions) and against our baseline — not reviewed in isolation.
2. The planted defects in the key are found for each revision (Rev 0 → Rev 1 → Rev 2), and the clean revision is called clean.
3. Direction matters: findings say who is late against whom, and what the contractual consequence is.
4. Retractions are clean: a challenged finding that was wrong is withdrawn with the reason, without undoing the correct ones.
