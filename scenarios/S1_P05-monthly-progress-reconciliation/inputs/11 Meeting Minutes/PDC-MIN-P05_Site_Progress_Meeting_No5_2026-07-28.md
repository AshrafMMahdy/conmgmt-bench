## 3. Progress round-table

**3.1 TP-001 Kallio (PK).** DH1 piling continuing. PK reported 64% complete against a programme
position of 66%. DH2 piling started on programme 05.07 and is at 38%. DH3 started early and is at
22%. All pile caps and ground beams to DH2 complete as of 28.07, two days later than programme,
which PK attributed to a concrete supply failure on 22.07.

PK raised the NW corner material again (his notes N-01 and N-03). Below 3.0 m the material is not
the glacial till described in PDC-SS-001; it contains concrete and brick fragments. Production in
that zone has fallen from 3.0 to 1.8 piles per day. PK stated Kallio consider this to fall under
BOQ B.04 and have reserved their position on time and cost.

**MN** noted that the QS site measure taken 30–31.07 will govern for valuation purposes and that
her measure of DH1 piling does not agree with PK's return. PK to reconcile.

**3.2 TP-005 Ilmatek (SR).** SR reported 12% on the cooling spine pipework, comprising site
mobilisation, setting out, and offsite prefabrication of spool sections at Hyvinkää. SR stated the
offsite element is claimable under tender qualification Q7.

**MN** disputed that Q7 survives into the executed subcontract and asked SR to identify the clause
in PDC-SUB-005 Rev1 that permits payment for offsite materials. **Action SR.**

**TL** observed that TP-005 labour on site has not reached the level shown in the Rev1 programme
for this period. SR said additional pipefitters are mobilising in August.

**3.3 TP-008 Lehtinen (ML).** Access roads ahead of programme at 40%, assisted by dry weather.
Fencing posts delivered 29.07, fencing starts 17.08 per programme. ML repeated that the NE car
park cannot start while the temporary parking compound remains in place (risk R-09).

## 4. Programme
