## 1. Method

**Method declared — causation + entitlement.** For each head, we identify the load-bearing reason by reading the contemporaneous records (period reports, plant logs, zone access register, correspondence register, accepted baseline programme) and asking: (i) on the engineering evidence, can this event delay Section 1? (ii) on the contractual evidence, does the Subcontractor have an entitlement that survives the Subcontract's clauses?

**Causation (Heads A and D-time):** the activity on which the claimed delay sits (activity 118 / IL-1400) carries 87 working days of total float on the accepted TP-005 baseline and is not on the critical path; its predecessor (activity 102) is complete. The 3 wd Head D-time element sits inside that float and is absorbed.

**Entitlement (Heads B, C):** Head B fails because Subcontract Clause 1.3 is back-to-back and the upstream Main Contract notice has expired; Head C fails because the 21-day Subcontract Clause 4.3 notice bar is missed by 44 days.

**Liability (Head D-cost):** Head D cost is admitted on liability (plant log puts risk on the Contractor). Q4 EUR 12,400 is accepted on the records — the build-up is reasonable and evidenced on our side (gang size corroborated by Period 08 W41 labour return; plant log admits the cause).

**Non-head-attributable lines (Q5–Q10):** assessed on their own terms against the P05 progress return.

**Limitation declared.** The accepted TP-005 baseline is Rev1. Kallio's Rev1 is not accepted (Meeting No. 7, action A07-03, closed 13.10.2026 with findings). The TP-005 Rev2 baseline is not accepted (8 high-severity findings, P05 progress return §2.2).

**Activity ID cross-reference.** Activity "118" in the main-contract master programme is the same physical activity as "IL-1400" in Ilmatek's TP-005 Rev1 baseline.

## 5. Head D — TC-02 tower crane outage

### 5.1 What the claim says

Ilmatek claim that the TC-02 tower crane was out of service for 3 working days (05.10.2026 → 07.10.2026). They claim no time (the 3 wd are subsumed in Head A) and Q4 EUR 12,400 (standing time, 3 days × 8 pipefitters + 2 welders + supervision).

### 5.3 Load-bearing reasons — liability (cost) + causation (time)

Two distinct defences for two distinct elements.

**(i) Cost — liability admitted.** The plant log entry is in our own record. It puts the risk on the Contractor (us). PDC-LTR-005-009 confirmed the outage to Ilmatek without denial of liability. Notice is within the bar. We accept the cost.

**(ii) Time — causation defeats.** Ilmatek say the 3 wd time element is subsumed in Head A's window (21.09–22.10.2026). If Head A is rejected, those 3 days do not transfer to another head — they are absorbed in the 87 wd total float of activity 118 (not on the critical path, per Meeting No. 8 section 4 and the Baseline Float — TP-005 row). The period 05–07.10.2026 sits inside that float; it had no consequence for Section 1.

6. No rate is in our records. The figure is taken on the claimant's quantification because the event is ours and the build-up is sound.

## 10. Limitations — what would weaken each load-bearing reason

**Head A — causation.** The argument rests on the 87 wd total float of activity 118 in the accepted TP-005 baseline. If the baseline is revised in a way that removes activity 118's float or makes it critical, the argument weakens. There is no agreed Rev2 baseline at present. Mitigation: track any Rev2 issue from Ilmatek and re-evaluate.

**Head B — back-to-back.** The argument rests on the 28-day Main Contract notice position having expired. If an upstream notice is later issued and is held valid by the ER (or by an adjudicator / arbitrator), the back-to-back is unlocked. Mitigation: confirm before any further Head B correspondence that no upstream notice has issued.

**Head C — the bar.** The argument rests on there being no earlier ILM notice for Head C. If Ilmatek produce an earlier notice (e.g. a contemporaneous site instruction log entry), the bar argument falls. Mitigation: re-search before any further Head C correspondence.

**Head D — liability admitted.** The argument rests on the plant log entry. If Ilmatek dispute that the outage was our risk (e.g. they argue a parts-supplier default rather than our scheduled maintenance), the admission may be revisited. Mitigation: hold to the admission on the records; do not amplify.

**Head D — Q4 EUR 12,400 accepted.** The argument rests on the build-up being reasonable and evidenced on our side. If the QS reconciliation against the daily plant log or the Period 08 daily returns produces a materially different figure, the headline is reopened. Trigger: if reconciliation produces a figure below EUR 9,920 (80% of EUR 12,400) or above EUR 14,880 (120%), the headline is reopened.

**Q5–Q8 — under-resourced period.** The argument rests on the P05 progress return showing TP-005 Ilmatek at 5 operatives on site in W30/W31 against 11 planned. If a later period return shows full establishment running, the argument weakens for that later period. Mitigation: re-check against P06, P07, P08 returns before any further Q5–Q8 correspondence.

**Q9–Q10 — sums not yet due.** The argument rests on Q1–Q8 not being due. If any of Q1–Q8 is admitted, Q9 and Q10 may partially accrue on the admitted portion. Mitigation: if Q4 is admitted at EUR 12,400, Q9 (8%) would accrue at EUR 992 and Q10 (finance charges) would accrue on the EUR 12,400 from the date of admission. We have not included these in the assessed figure; they are noted here for completeness.

**Fallback positions are not documented.** Per the discipline agreed at plan stage, each head has one load-bearing reason and no stack of fallbacks. If any of the above weakens, the head falls back on whatever the next-best entitlement is — but those are not pre-built into this assessment.
