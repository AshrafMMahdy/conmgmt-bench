### Head A — Late release DH2 slab (22 working days claimed; Q1 EUR 49,280)

**Assessed: 0 working days / EUR 0.**

The activity you identify as delayed — activity IL-1400 in your Rev1 baseline, equivalent to activity 118 in the main-contract master programme — carries 87 working days of total float on the accepted TP-005 baseline and is not on the critical path. This is recorded on the Baseline Float — TP-005 sheet of the Period 08 records, and confirmed by the Contractor's planning lead at Meeting No. 8 on 27 October 2026 (section 4: *"TP-005 activity 118 is not on the critical path"*). Activity 118's predecessor in the accepted baseline is activity 102 (DH2 plant room builders work openings, complete 2026-09-04, QS-measured 100%), not activity 63 (DH2 ground floor slab). The slab slip cannot, on the records, have delayed activity 118 in any way that affects Section 1.

We accept the slab for the DH2 north bay was released late; we do not accept that this delayed Section 1.

### Head B — 110 kV revised energisation (14 working days claimed; Q2 EUR 31,360)

**Assessed: 0 working days / EUR 0.**

The 110 kV slip is a main-contract event under the Employer's risk. Subcontract Clause 1.3 entitles the Subcontractor only to the extent the Contractor recovers from the Employer in respect of the same matter. On the Main Contract, the 28-day notice position under Sub-Clause 20.2.1 (restored to 28 days in PDC-PC-001 RevC) expired on 25 September 2026. The Contractor's notice was not issued. Nothing has been recovered from the Employer, and on the present record nothing can be recovered. Under Clause 1.3, no entitlement arises.

We have considered the reference in your covering letter to Clause 6.1. Clause 6.1 governs payment timing; Clause 1.3 governs entitlement. They operate on different stages of the same chain, and Clause 6.1 does not displace Clause 1.3.

### Head C — NW zone withdrawn (6 working days claimed; Q3 EUR 13,440)

**Assessed: 0 working days / EUR 0.**

We accept the NW zone was withdrawn on 12 August 2026 and returned on 19 August 2026 (6 working days), on our instruction EI-004. The withdrawal is acknowledged on the Zone Access Register of the Period 08 records, signed by your supervisor J. Virtanen on 12.08.2026.

Subcontract Clause 4.3 requires the Subcontractor to notify the Contractor within 21 days of becoming aware of the event, and provides that if notice is not given within that period the Subcontractor loses its entitlement to time and money in respect of that event. The 21-day bar on the NW zone withdrawal expired on 2 September 2026. Your first notice of Head C is within this claim, dated 16 October 2026 — 65 days after the event, 44 days past the bar. Clause 4.3 is engaged; the entitlement under Head C is lost.

### Head D — TC-02 tower crane outage (0 working days claimed; Q4 EUR 12,400 cost only)

**Assessed: 0 working days / EUR 12,400 accepted.**

We accept the 3-day outage of TC-02 (05–07.10.2026) and the cost to your pipefitting and welding gang. Our plant log records the outage as the Contractor's own maintenance and accepts it as the Contractor's risk. PDC-LTR-005-009 of 06.10.2026 confirmed the outage to you without denial of liability. Your notice ILM-NOT-005 of 08.10.2026 was within the 21-day Clause 4.3 bar (the bar runs from the outage start on 05.10.2026 and expires on 26.10.2026).

The 3 working days time element is absorbed in the float of activity 118 (see Head A above). The outage window sits inside the 87 working days of total float of an activity that is not on the critical path; the period had no consequence for Section 1.

On quantum, we accept your Q4 build-up of EUR 12,400 (3 days × 8 pipefitters + 2 welders + supervision). Our Period 08 W41 labour return corroborates the gang size (8 pipefitters, 3 welders, 1 supervisor on site that week; the welder count differs by 1, which is immaterial). The plant log admits the cause. We will pay EUR 12,400 against your next application.
