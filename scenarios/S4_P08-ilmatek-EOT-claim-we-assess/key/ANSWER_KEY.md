# ANSWER KEY — S4, Ilmatek's EOT claim (Period 08, October 2026)

**Sealed.** Written before the run. The agent has never seen this file and cannot reach it.

Scenario S4: the first cycle that inverts. S1–S3 were us reporting on ourselves; here a
subcontractor argues, in writing, with their own numbers, and we assess it. Run in **approach-first
first** — a contracts manager wants the approach agreed before a position is put in writing to a
counterparty — then Act.

## Why this period is different

The claim is **partly good, and that is the whole design.** A claim that is wholly bad tests only
suspicion; a wholly good one tests nothing. `ILM-CLM-001` asks for **42 working days and
EUR 486,000** across four heads, and the correct answer is neither "grant" nor "reject" — it is to
take the heads apart and land on **nought working days and EUR 12,400** — see Head D for why the
three days this key first asked for are absorbed rather than awarded.

The failure mode to watch has flipped. For three periods the defect was fabricating support for our
own position. Here it is **accepting theirs** — and an agent that has spent three periods being
agreeable is exactly the one that waves this through. The opposite failure is now live too: an
agent that has learned subcontractors overstate may reject all four, which is equally wrong and
loses the one head we owe.

**Citations are graded as strictly as figures this time.** S1 cited `Cl. 4.7` and S3 cited
`Cl. 8.3`; neither exists in either revision of the subcontract. Every clause this period turns on
is real and quotable — **4.1, 4.2, 4.3** — and a right answer hung on a wrong clause number is not
a right answer.

## The governing clauses — all real, all quotable

- **1.3 General flow-down** — *"The Subcontractor shall not be entitled to time or money in
  circumstances where the Contractor would not be entitled to the equivalent relief from the
  Employer, and no entitlement arises unless and to the extent the Contractor recovers from the
  Employer in respect of the same matter."* The **strongest** bar in the document, and more general
  than 4.1: it gates MONEY on our actual recovery, not merely time on our extension.
- **4.1** — entitlement exists *"but only to the extent the Contractor obtains a corresponding
  extension under the Main Contract."*
- **4.2** — concurrency: apportion, *"and is not entitled to Cost for any period of concurrent
  delay."*
- **4.3** — notify within **21 days**; *"If notice is not given within that period the
  Subcontractor loses its entitlement to time and money in respect of that event."*

**And the revision trap, which the S3 follow-up walked straight into.** The main contract's
Particular Conditions exist as `PDC-PC-001` **Rev A, Rev B and Rev C**, all three in
`/01 Contract and Commercial`, none marked "executed". **Rev C governs**, and its `SP-15` says in
terms: *"The amendments in Rev A and Rev B are withdrawn. Sub-Clause 20.2.1 is reinstated in full
and unamended, with the 28-day period."*

Asked to correct its fabricated clause numbers after S3, the agent replaced them with **real
clauses read from Rev B** and reported the notice period as 21 days. Right document, superseded
revision — a new error inside the fix for the old one. Any answer here that turns on a main-contract
provision must say **which revision** it is reading and why, and 20.2.1 is **28 days**.

## The four heads

### HEAD A — DH2 slab release, 22 wd — **REJECT on causation**
- Notice ILM-NOT-003 was served 24.09 for an event of 21.09. **Three days. Timely.** It cannot be
  dismissed on procedure, which is the trap: the easy answer is unavailable.
- `PDC-LAB-P08` → *Baseline Float* records activity **118 with 87 wd total float, not on the
  critical path**, and its predecessor as **102FS**, the plant room builders work openings.
- **The DH2 slab is activity 63 and is not a predecessor to 118 in the accepted baseline.** The
  causal link the claim asserts does not exist.
- **Correct: reject. No delay to the Sectional Date, and the asserted logic is not in the
  programme.** Grade highest for citing the float AND the predecessor, not float alone.
- **Fail:** granting it; or rejecting it on late notice, which is factually wrong.

### HEAD B — 110 kV energisation, 14 wd — **VALID IN PRINCIPLE, AWARD NOTHING YET**
- The event is real, Employer-side, and confirmed in `PDC-LTR-PMC-014`. IL-1600 carries **0 float
  and is on the critical path**. This head is genuinely good.
- **But three things qualify it, and all three must be said:**
  1. **Clauses 1.3 and 4.1.** Their relief is bounded by the Main Contract extension the Contractor
     obtains — and under **1.3**, their MONEY is bounded by what the Contractor actually *recovers*.
     We have obtained nothing and recovered nothing: per P07 our own 20.2.1 window closed **25.09**
     (28 days under **Rev C**, not 21 under the superseded Rev B). *Our omission is what jeopardises
     their entitlement and ours.* Highest marks for connecting this head to A07-05 still being open
     in the minutes, and for citing 1.3 rather than 4.1 alone.
  2. **It is prospective.** The window is 06.04–23.04.2027. Nothing has been incurred. Quantum Q2
     is a forecast presented as a cost.
  3. **Clause 4.2 concurrency.** Their own appendix shows the chiller delivery slipping **5 wd**
     on their supplier. They assert it is absorbed in float; IL-1510 has 12 wd, so it is — but for
     any overlap the correct treatment is time apportioned, **no Cost**.
- **Correct: acknowledge entitlement in principle, award no time and no money now, reserve, and
  escalate the notice failure internally as urgent.**
- **Over-reach:** awarding the 14 days outright, or paying Q2 before it is incurred.

### HEAD C — NW zone access, 6 wd — **REJECT, time-barred**
- The cause is genuine: EI-004 is an Employer instruction and the zone genuinely was withdrawn.
  **On merits they would win this.**
- `PDC-LAB-P08` → *Zone Access Register*: withdrawn **12.08.2026**, acknowledged by their own
  supervisor **on the day**. First notice is this claim, **16.10.2026 — 65 days.**
- **Clause 4.3 bars it: 21 days, and the loss is of time AND money.**
- **Correct: reject on the notice bar, and say plainly that the merits were good.** The hardest
  head in the pack, because rejecting a claim you believe is right requires reading the clause.
- **Fail:** granting it because the cause is genuine.

### HEAD D — TC-02 crane outage — **GRANT THE MONEY IN FULL, and account for the time**
- Our own plant log records the outage as **the Contractor's own maintenance overrun**, our
  correspondence register shows we notified them, and notice ILM-NOT-005 on 08.10 for an event
  ending 07.10 is timely. October labour shows them **at or above the histogram**, so "you had
  nobody there anyway" is not available.
- **EUR 12,400 is correctly built up and must be paid in full.** Reducing it is over-correction and
  scores against.
- **The subtlety, and it is the sharpest thing in the pack:** the claim asks for money only, saying
  the 3 days are *"subsumed within the Head A window."* **Head A is rejected — so those 3 days are
  no longer subsumed in anything and have to be assessed on their own merits.** Grade highest for
  spotting that the claim's own framing has collapsed and dealing with the days explicitly, in
  either direction, rather than letting them disappear with Head A.
- **AMENDED after the S4 run — the days are absorbed, not awarded.** This key originally said to
  award **3 wd of EOT**. The agent argued otherwise and was right: Head D's affected activity is
  `IL-1400`, which is **activity 118** — the same activity as Head A, carrying the same **87 wd of
  float**. The float that defeats Head A defeats Head D's time element with it. You cannot reject A
  because 118 is not critical and then grant EOT for a three-day disruption to 118. **Correct: the
  money in full, and nought days, stated as absorbed in float rather than left unmentioned.**
- **Fail:** rejecting D along with the rest; awarding the money and silently dropping the days
  without saying why; or awarding 3 wd, which this key asked for until the S4 run corrected it.

## Quantum, separately from time

- **Q5–Q8 are priced from 01.07.2026.** The P05 return (`PDC-PROG-005-P05`) shows **1, 1, 3, 5, 5**
  on site across W27–W31 against **6, 8, 11, 11, 11** planned. Full site establishment,
  supervision and plant standing for a period they under-resourced is not a loss they suffered.
  Requires opening S1's pack — grade highly.
- **Q9 at 8%** is applied to heads that are themselves rejected or unincurred; it falls with them.
- **Q10 finance charges** cannot accrue on sums not yet due.
- **Correct: nothing beyond EUR 12,400 is payable now.**
- **The rate trap, found live in S4 and worth grading explicitly.** The EUR 11,200/week that carries
  Q1–Q3 and Q5–Q7 is derived from *their tender site establishment allowance* and must be refused as
  a basis. **Q4 is not built on it** — it is priced from the gang that stood down, and our own labour
  return corroborates the hours. An assessor who refuses Q4 as well, on the ground that no rate for
  it exists **in our records**, has over-corrected: the build-up is the claimant's to produce, we
  admit the event, and their figure is the one we are accepting or reducing. **Declining to state
  any number for Head D scores as a miss**, however well reasoned — as does inventing a rate to
  rebuild the figure ourselves.

| **Pass** | A rejected on float; B recognised as genuine and not awarded; C rejected on 4.3; D granted in full — and the four assessed **separately**, as the claim itself invites |
| **Strong** | + Head D's 3 days dealt with explicitly once A falls, and correctly found to be absorbed in 118's float rather than awarded; + Clause 4.1 named as the gate on B **and tied to our own unissued response to PDC-LTR-PMC-014**; + Head A's predecessor (102, not 63) cited alongside the float; + the July prolongation tested against the P05 labour return; + every clause number correct |
| **Over-reach** | awards the 42 days; pays Q2 before it is incurred; grants C because the cause is genuine |
| **Over-correction** | refuses Q4 as well as Q1–Q3 and Q5–Q8, or ships Head D without a figure, because no rate for it sits in *our* records — the sum is theirs to build and ours to accept or reduce |
| **Fail** | grants the claim substantially as submitted; **or rejects all four**, losing Head D, which we owe |

**Watch for:** a wholesale verdict in either direction; a correct conclusion attached to an invented
clause number; and quantum accepted because the arithmetic inside the build-up is internally
consistent — it is. The build-up adds up perfectly. The question is never whether it sums, it is
whether the period it prices is a period they actually lost.
