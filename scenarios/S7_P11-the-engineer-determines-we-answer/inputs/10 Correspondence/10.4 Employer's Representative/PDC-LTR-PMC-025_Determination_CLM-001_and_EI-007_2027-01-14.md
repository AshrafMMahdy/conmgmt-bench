## 1. PDC-CLM-001 — extension of time

The Contractor claims **30 working days**. The Engineer determines **21 working days**.

The Contractor's own Concurrent Delay Record discloses that the L4 structural handover by TP-001
Kallio was **9 working days late** and that this delay affected activity ST-1310,
which is common to both delay events. The Employer's risk event and the Contractor's risk event
therefore overlap to that extent. Applying Sub-Clause 8.5 as amended by SP-7, the Engineer
apportions the overlap to the Contractor and reduces the extension accordingly:

> 30 wd claimed, less 9 wd concurrent Contractor delay = **21 wd determined.**

The Contractor's Time Impact Analysis is noted. The Engineer does not accept that the L4 delay is
non-critical.

## 2. PDC-CLM-001 — prolongation cost

The Engineer accepts the Contractor's rate of **EUR 4,492.68 per working day**, derived
from priced Bill Section A, as the correct measured basis.

Applied to the 21 working days determined above, the Engineer determines prolongation cost
in the sum of **EUR 107,824.32**.
