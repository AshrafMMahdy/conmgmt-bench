| Purpose | Operative wording of Sub-Clause 3.7.5 under the executed Rev C Particular Conditions |

Rev C makes no amendment to Sub-Clause 3.7. The General Conditions apply unamended. The operative
text of **Sub-Clause 3.7.5 [Dissatisfaction with Engineer's determination]** is:

> "If either Party is dissatisfied with a determination of the Engineer under this Sub-Clause 3.7,
> that Party may give a **NOD** to the other Party, with a copy to the Engineer, **within 28 days**
> after receiving it, stating that it is a Notice of Dissatisfaction with the Engineer's
> determination and setting out the reasons for the dissatisfaction.
>
> **If the Engineer's determination is not revised and no NOD is given within the period of 28 days
> stated above, the determination shall be deemed to have been accepted by both Parties and shall be
> final and binding on both Parties.**"

Note for the file: the 28 days runs from **receipt**, not from the date on the determination.

Note for the file: a NOD does not have to resolve the dispute. It has to be given in time, identify
the determination, and set out the reasons. Reasons may be developed afterwards.
