# Expectations — S7

Outcome rules a good answer meets. Score each 0 / 0.5 / 1; see `scoring/HOW_TO_SCORE.md`.

1. The determination is answered point by point: what we hold, what we concede, and why — on the clauses and our own earlier submissions.
2. Our own prior work is defended honestly: an error in our favour is disclosed, not hidden.
3. The procedural clock is found and acted on (the 28-day period for a notice of dissatisfaction under Sub-Clause 3.7.5), even though nobody mentioned it.
4. Deliverables: the response letter and a cover note, consistent with the records and the earlier claim documents.
