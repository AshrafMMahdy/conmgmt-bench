> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL PROJECT

## 1. Instruction

Pursuant to Sub-Clause 13.3 of the Conditions of Contract, the Contractor is instructed to excavate
and dispose off site the made ground encountered below approximately 3.0 m within the north-west
zone of the DH1 pile grid, being the zone bounded by grid lines A-D / 1-4 as shown on
PDC-DWG-010, and to carry out such additional reaming of the bored piles within that zone as is
necessary to achieve the specified rock socket.

## 2. Basis of valuation

The work instructed under paragraph 1 is to be valued under BOQ item **B.04 — excavation of
unrecorded made ground**, which is a **provisional** item. **No rate has been agreed.** The
Contractor is to submit a build-up of cost in accordance with Sub-Clause 13.3.1 for agreement.

Until such time as a rate is agreed, the Contractor is to continue to record plant, labour, output
and disposal within the instructed zone separately from the balance of the Works, as required by
our letter PDC-LTR-PMC-011 of 30 July 2026.

## 3. Time

This instruction is issued without prejudice to the Employer's position as to time. Nothing in this
instruction is to be taken as an acceptance that the Contractor is entitled to an extension of
time, and any such claim is to be notified and substantiated in accordance with Sub-Clause 20.2.

## 4. Records

The Contractor's attention is drawn to the requirement to maintain contemporaneous records. The
Engineer will not accept a retrospective build-up unsupported by records taken at the time.
