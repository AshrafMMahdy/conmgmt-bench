**To:** Main Contractor, Polaris DC-1
**From:** Anna Härkönen, Employer's Representative
**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Subject:** 110 kV grid connection — revised energisation date

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL PROJECT

Dear Sirs,

Further to paragraph 2 of our letter PDC-LTR-PMC-011 dated 30 July 2026, and to item 5 of the
minutes of Site Progress Meeting No. 6, we write to notify you formally of the position advised to
the Employer by Vantaan Energia Oy.

**1. Revised date**

The energisation date for the 110 kV connection, previously confirmed as **12 March 2027** in the
grid connection offer PDC-GC-001, has been revised to **24 April 2027**.

Vantaan Energia have attributed the revision to the reprogramming of the regional outage window
following works on the Kehä III feeder. The revised date is stated by them to be firm.

**2. Action required of the Contractor**

You are requested to advise, within 14 days, the consequence of the revised date for the accepted
programme, and in particular for the Section 1 commissioning sequence.

Any claim for an extension of time arising from this matter is to be notified and substantiated in
accordance with the Conditions of Contract. This letter is a notification of fact and is not an
admission of entitlement.

**3. Other matters**

The Employer's consideration of the main gate security provision is continuing. No instruction
arises at this stage and the Contractor should not incur cost in anticipation.
