# Expectations — PRE-08

Outcome rules a good answer meets. Score each 0 / 0.5 / 1; see `scoring/HOW_TO_SCORE.md`.

1. Item-by-item: for every finding of the Rev 0 review, Rev 1 is checked — resolved, mitigated, argued, or still open — with the clause that resolves it.
2. Judgement, not ticking: items resolved in substance but not in form (the YSE 1998 label; the bounded commencement) are reasoned, not marked either way without reasons.
3. New consequences of the new drafting are caught (the 21-day vs 28-day notice; stacked securities).
4. A clear verdict and the short list of points to raise before signature.
