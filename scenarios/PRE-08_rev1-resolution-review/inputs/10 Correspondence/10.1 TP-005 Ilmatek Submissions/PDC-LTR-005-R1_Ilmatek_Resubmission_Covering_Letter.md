Dear Sir,

We have adopted every point in your review of Rev 0. We accept that the Rev 0
programme was not built on PDC-CAL-001, did not show the Sectional Dates, did not
reflect PDC-LT-001, assumed simultaneous plantroom access, and put the waste-heat
connection outside the heating season. Those were our errors and Rev 1 corrects
all of them.

**We must, however, raise a matter arising directly from your instruction.**

Your review also directed us to lengthen our installation and commissioning
durations. Specifically, that 35 days for four centrifugal chillers was "at best a
third of what is needed", and that 10 days for L4 and L5 commissioning should be
25–35 days. We have adopted both, in full, as instructed.

Applying those durations to your own procurement dates produces the following for
Section 1:

| Step | Basis | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Chillers — order by | PDC-LT-001 LT-07 | 7 August 2026 |
| Chillers — on site | LT-07, 26 weeks | **5 February 2027** |
| Chiller installation | Per your review, 10 weeks | 15 April 2027 |
| Static completion, Section 1 | 2 weeks | 29 April 2027 |
| Commissioning L4, Hall 1 | Per your review | 20 May 2027 |
| Commissioning L5, Hall 1 (witnessed) | | **6 August 2027** |
| **Sectional Date 1 required** | PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1 Part 1 | **2 April 2027** |

The window between the chiller delivery date fixed by your own lead-time register
and Sectional Date 1 is **eight weeks**. The durations your review requires occupy
approximately seventeen. Sections 2, 3 and 4 are achieved comfortably; Section 1
cannot be, on your figures.

We are not asking you to reinstate our Rev 0 durations, which we accept were
optimistic. We are saying that the instruction and the Sectional Date cannot both
be satisfied, and that this is not a matter we can resolve on our own initiative.

**We see three ways forward and are content with any of them:**

1. **Reconcile the durations.** Your review's figures were professional judgement
   rather than measured rates. If Hall 1 chiller installation and commissioning can
   be agreed at durations consistent with the eight-week window your own register
   implies, Section 1 is achievable and we will resubmit accordingly.
2. **Acceleration.** We can resource Hall 1 to a shift pattern and achieve 2 April,
   subject to agreement on the cost, which we estimate at EUR 340,000.
3. **Vary Sectional Date 1.** If Hall 1 Taking-Over can move, we will meet any date
   from 15 May 2027 without acceleration.

Rev 1 as submitted shows the position honestly: Section 1 is marked NOT ACHIEVED
against IL-1800 rather than constrained to a date we cannot deliver. We would
rather show you the problem now than programme around it and claim it later.

We would be grateful for your direction. The chiller order must be placed by
7 August 2026 under LT-07, which is now imminent, and that date is not within our
control to extend.

Yours faithfully,
