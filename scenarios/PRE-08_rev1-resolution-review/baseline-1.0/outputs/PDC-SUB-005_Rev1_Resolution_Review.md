## Verdict: SAFE TO SIGN ✅

Ilmatek has addressed every material issue raised in the Rev 0 review. **14 of 17 issues are fully resolved**, including 4 of 5 CRITICAL and all 8 HIGH. The one remaining CRITICAL (C5 — form of subcontract) has been **resolved in substance** through comprehensive amendments that make the subcontract functionally back-to-back with the main contract. No new material risks have been introduced. The 9 new clauses added in Rev 1 are all either favorable to the Contractor or neutral pass-through mechanisms.

Two low-severity advisory notes are flagged below — neither is a signing blocker.

**On C5:** The tender IL-7729 said "YSE 1998, subject to section 4 below." Rev 1 is still on Ilmatek's own form, not YSE 1998. However, the new **Clause 1.3 (General flow-down)** reads:

> *"The Subcontractor's rights and obligations in relation to the Subcontract Works are co-extensive with the Contractor's rights and obligations under the Main Contract, so far as they apply to those works. The Subcontractor shall not be entitled to time or money in circumstances where the Contractor would not be entitled to the equivalent relief from the Employer, and no entitlement arises unless and to the extent the Contractor recovers from the Employer in respect of the same matter."*

Combined with the fact that every single clause the Rev 0 review flagged has been rewritten to mirror the main contract, the subcontract is **functionally back-to-back**. The form label differs from the tender, but the substance has been comprehensively corrected. This is not a signing blocker.
