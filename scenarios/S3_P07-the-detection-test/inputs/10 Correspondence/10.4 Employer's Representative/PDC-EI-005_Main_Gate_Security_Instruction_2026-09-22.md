# PDC-EI-005 — Engineer's Instruction

**Project:** Polaris DC-1 — Aviapolis Data Campus, Vantaa
**To:** The Contractor
**Instruction no.:** EI-005
**Date:** 22 September 2026

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL PROJECT

## 1. Instruction

Pursuant to Sub-Clause 13.3, the Contractor is instructed to increase the security provision at the
main gate, comprising: an additional vehicle barrier and search bay; upgrade of the gatehouse
access control to the standard described in PDC-DWG-016; and provision of two additional
fixed-position cameras with associated cabling and integration to the security head end.

## 2. Quotation

The Contractor is to submit a quotation within 28 days in accordance with Sub-Clause 13.3.1,
comprising a build-up of cost and a statement of any effect on the programme.

**No rates have been agreed for this work.** The BOQ contains rates for the original gate scope at
Section I; the Engineer expresses no view as to whether those rates are applicable to the work
instructed here, and the Contractor is not to assume that they are.

## 3. Time

This instruction is issued without prejudice to the Employer's position as to time.
