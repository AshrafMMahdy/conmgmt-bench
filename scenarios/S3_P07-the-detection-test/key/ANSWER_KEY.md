## Planted findings

### H1 — the cost submission double-counts what is already recorded
- Kallio submit **EUR 214,600** for the EI-004 works, "executed 05.08.2026 to 31.08.2026".
- The P06 CVR already records **EUR 138,400 incurred** under `C-1140` for that same work.
- The quantities are **identical** — 1,840 m³ excavation, 1,840 m³ disposal, 118 hr reaming — they
  are the same works priced differently, not additional works.
- **Correct: one liability, not two. Total exposure EUR 214,600; the additional over what is already
  recorded is EUR 76,200.** The P07 CVR says so in terms: *"This is one liability, not two."*
- **Fail:** adding them to report ~EUR 353,000 (a 65% overstatement).
- **Second layer, needs P05:** line 5, *"standing time, piling rig 2, 6 days, EUR 14,700"* is the
  **hydraulic failure** in Kallio's own P05 note N-02, which the P06 action log closed as
  *"Kallio supply-chain risk… no Employer liability."* **That line must be rejected outright.**
- Grade highest for both layers; Pass for the double-count alone.

### H2 — a completion certificate signed on a day nobody was on site
- `KMO-QC-0219` certifies bays W3/W4, "inspected and signed **2026-09-20**".
- **20.09.2026 is a Sunday.** The gate log gives daily detail: **19 and 20.09, TP-001 = 0**, one
  security officer. The site diary reads *"Site closed. Weekend security cover only."*
- The certificate is also **"not witnessed — main contractor representative unavailable."**
- Independently, the **QS measures activity 59 at 90%**: bay W4 upstands incomplete, ~24 m.
- **Correct: the certificate is not valid evidence of completion; activity 59 is 90%, not 100%.**
- **The consequence is the real finding:** the DH2 ground floor slab (activity 63) commenced 21.09
  **over the bay whose waterproofing is incomplete** — the QS measure says so explicitly. That is a
  defect being built over, not a valuation dispute. Grade highest for reaching that.

### H3 — a revised programme that hides the slip in a successor
- `PDC-SCH-001 Rev1` covering note ¶3: *"The revision reflects progress only. There is no change to
  the date for completion of Section 1 and no extension of time is sought."*
- Against **Rev0**, which is in the project folder, exactly two things changed on **activity 63**:
  - duration **22 wd → 15 wd**
  - predecessor **59FS → 59SS+5**
- Both changes are on a **0-float, critical** activity. The link change lets the slab start before
  the waterproofing beneath it finishes — which is precisely what physically happened, and precisely
  what H2 shows to be a defect.
- **Correct: the completion date is preserved on paper by compressing a successor and weakening a
  link, not by recovering work. Rev1 should not be accepted as the programme of record.** The
  minutes support this: TL has *not* accepted it.
- **Fail:** accepting ¶3 at face value, or reporting "TP-001 has submitted Rev1, no change to
  completion".

### H4 — a delay claim against an activity with 87 days of float
- `ILM-NOT-003` claims **22 working days** on activity **118**, blaming late release of the DH2 slab.
- Activity 118 carries **87 days of total float** in the accepted baseline and is **not critical**.
  Its predecessor is **102**, not 63 — the DH2 slab is not its predecessor at all.
- Ilmatek's own progress is **fine**: 48% claimed against 46% measured, labour at or above the Rev1
  histogram all month.
- **Correct: a delay to activity 118 is not a delay to completion. The notice is acknowledged and
  the entitlement rejected on float, and the causal link asserted does not exist in the programme.**
- **Fail:** granting it, passing it through, or reporting it as a threat to Section 1.

### H5 — the notice **we** failed to give (the hardest, and the one that costs money)
- `PDC-LTR-PMC-014` (28.08.2026) required the Contractor to advise the programme consequence
  **within 14 days** — i.e. by **11.09.2026**.
- The **outgoing correspondence register** in our own record lists every letter issued. There is no
  response to PMC-014. The last relevant entry is 24.09 about EI-005.
- The minutes §5 record AH asking the question and noting **"no response has been received"**, and
  reminding the meeting that any claim is subject to the notice provisions.
- Under **FIDIC Yellow Book 2017 Sub-Clause 20.2.1** the claim notice period is **28 days from
  awareness**: 28.08 + 28 days = **25.09.2026**. At the data date that window **closed five days
  ago**.
- **Correct: we are out of time on our own claim for an Employer-side event, and it is our
  omission.** This is the one finding that is about the absence of our own document rather than a
  defect in somebody else's, and an agent reading only what it was given will not look for it.
- Grade **highest in the period** for finding it; **Strong** for finding it and naming the date the
  window closed; Pass-level reports may still miss it, but a report that discusses the 110 kV
  position **without** noticing the missing response has missed the commercially serious item.
