## 3. Substantive response

We are modelling the time impact of the revised energisation date on the Section 1 commissioning sequence (Milestone MS-06, 02 May 2027) and the dependent activities. Our substantive response, including any consequential application under the Conditions of Contract, will be issued by **31 October 2026** and will be substantiated in accordance with the Conditions of Contract.

Pending that substantive response, we confirm that the Contractor continues to perform the Works in accordance with the accepted programme and the accepted programme amendments to the extent that performance is not rendered impossible or substantially more onerous by the revised energisation date.

**Important constraint on the substance response.** PDC-PC-001 RevC SP-7 / Sub-Clause 8.5 replaces the RevB "concurrent delay bar" with an **apportionment regime**: *"Where a delay for which the Contractor is responsible is concurrent with a delay for which the Contractor would otherwise be entitled to an extension of time, the Engineer shall apportion the delay between the concurrent causes on a fair and reasonable basis, having regard to the contemporary records. The Contractor shall be entitled to an extension of time for the apportioned share attributable to the Employer's risk events, **and shall not be entitled to Cost for any period of concurrent delay**."* Any extension of time that may be due to the Contractor under Sub-Clause 8.5 in respect of the revised energisation date does not, of itself, give rise to any entitlement to Cost under Sub-Clause 8.5 or otherwise, where the Contractor is concurrently delayed by matters within its own control or within the control of any subcontractor. The Contractor's present record includes concurrently-delayed matters in TP-001 Kallio (earthworks / piling cost over-run) and TP-005 Ilmatek (productivity record), which are recorded in the Period 07 progress report and will be addressed in the substance response.

**Notice position — operative PC is RevC.** PDC-PC-001 RevC SP-15 reinstates Sub-Clause 20.2.1 in full and unamended, with the **28-day period**. The 110 kV slip event became known on 28 August 2026; the 28-day clock expired on 25 September 2026. **The Contractor is therefore out of time on a Sub-Clause 20.2.1 notice for the 110 kV slip event itself.** However, PDC-PC-001 RevC SP-15 also reinstates Sub-Clause 20.2.2 in full, including the Engineer's obligation to give notice of any non-compliance and the consequences of failing to do so. The Contractor reserves its position under Sub-Clause 20.2.2 in respect of any notice of non-compliance that the Engineer may have given, or failed to give, in respect of the 110 kV slip event. Separately, the DH2 ground-floor slab pour 1 event of 21 September 2026 gives a separate, still-open 28-day window (expires 19 October 2026) for any EoT application related to that event; the Contractor will consider whether to anchor any EoT application on that event.

### Notes on issue (for the contracts team — not for transmission)

1. **This letter is overdue.** The 14-day window the ER set closed on 11 September 2026, 19 days before the data date. The Period 07 progress report (P07 cycle 5) records this gap plainly and does not say the letter has been issued. The contracts team should consider whether to draw the gap to the ER's attention in the cover note or whether to let the substance speak.

2. **Why hold-harmless and not a substantive response.** The revised date is 8 days before Section 1 (02.05.2027). Whether that creates an EoT entitlement depends on (a) whether the revision is a Compensation Event under Sub-Clause 8.5 (RevC SP-7), (b) the critical-path position post-modelling, and (c) the contractual position of any concurrent delay. That modelling is not done. A substantive response before the modelling is complete would risk conceding or denying before we know the answer.

3. **Reference number.** This letter has been drafted under the reference number PDC-LTR-001-021 to align with the outgoing-correspondence register. The register should be updated by the document controller on issue. **The draft does not assume the letter has been issued; the Period 07 report records the gap, not the issue.**

4. **Distribution.** Internal only at this stage — PD; PMC; document controller. Transmission to the ER by the contracts team on issue.

5. **Apportionment regime — paragraph 3.** The reference to PDC-PC-001 RevC SP-7 / Sub-Clause 8.5 apportionment is the materially new point in this cycle. RevB's "concurrent delay bar stands" modification was withdrawn by RevC; RevC replaces it with an apportionment regime. The Cost bar is retained (no Cost for concurrent delay). The Contractor can still get a partial EoT for the Employer's risk share of any concurrent period, but only if the 28-day notice has been served in time, or with a credible 20.2.2 argument if the Engineer didn't flag late-notice.

6. **Notice position — paragraph 3.** The 28-day notice period applies in full under RevC SP-15. The 110 kV slip event became known 28.08.2026; the 28-day clock expired 25.09.2026. The Contractor is out of time on a Sub-Clause 20.2.1 notice for the 110 kV slip event itself. The 21.09.2026 slab commencement event gives a separate, still-open 28-day window (expires 19.10.2026) for any EoT application related to that event. The contracts team should consider whether to anchor any EoT application on the slab event rather than the 110 kV event.

7. **Sub-Clause references — corrected in P07 cycle 5.** The operative PC is PDC-PC-001 RevC (FINAL, 24.02.2026), not RevB. The previous draft (cycle 4) cited RevB provisions that RevC has withdrawn. The correct references are SP-7 / Sub-Clause 8.5 (apportionment), SP-9 / Sub-Clause 13.3.1 (interim valuation), SP-12 / Sub-Clause 14.6 (set-off limited), SP-15 / Sub-Clause 20.2.1 (28-day notice), SP-15 / Sub-Clause 20.2.2 (reinstated in full).
