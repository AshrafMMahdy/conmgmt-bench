## 1. What was certified

On 18 September 2026 Kallio (TP-001) certified the DH2 ground-floor slab waterproofing (BOQ item C-1180) as 100% complete for bays W3 and W4, by signing inspection request **KMO-QC-0219** and notifying the Contractor accordingly. The certification covered the full scope of C-1180 as defined in the Kallio method statement and the BOQ: slab sub-base, perimeter upstands, day joints, and penetrations.

The Kallio signing date is **20 September 2026** — two days after the claimed completion date of 18 September 2026. Per Kallio's own record on the P07 progress return, **the certificate was not witnessed by the Contractor**. The Contractor representative was, per Kallio's own record, "unavailable" on the certification date.

This was recorded in Kallio's Period 07 progress return PDC-PROG-001-P07, row 59, with a value of 100% cum and a completion date of 18.09.2026. The KS-Kallio interim application was raised on the basis of the 100% certification.

The earlier Kallio certificate **KMO-QC-0207** (covering bays W1 and W2, dated 20.08.2026) was witnessed by the Contractor and is accepted on its merits; no open issue against W1 + W2.

## 2. What was measured

The Contractor's site team, with the Contractor's Quantity Surveyor (MN), carried out a joint walk of the DH2 ground-floor slab on **28, 29 and 30 September 2026**. The surveyor has recorded the following non-conformance with the Kallio certification of 18.09.2026:

- **Bay W4, north edge upstands — approximately 24 m of perimeter upstand remain incomplete.** The upstands are required to be a minimum 300 mm above the finished slab level, formed at the slab-to-wall transition, with continuity of the waterproofing membrane sealed to the upstand.
- The defect extends across the linear north edge of bay W4 from approximately chainage 14.0 m to chainage 38.0 m (24 lineal metres, full width of the north edge).
- The membrane and protection layer are in place but not sealed at the upstand; the upstand itself is not formed on the affected length.
- Day joints, penetrations and other items covered by KMO-QC-0219 are otherwise in order.

The Contractor's site diary records the absence of the upstand works on the affected length on 21.09.2026 (the day of DH2 slab pour 1 of 4). The QS-measured cumulative percentage for C-1180 is therefore **90% cum**, not 100% as certified.

## 3. The certification — assessed

KMO-QC-0219 is **not worth the paper it is written on**. The reasons:

- **The certificate was not witnessed by the Contractor.** Kallio's own record on the P07 progress return states "not witnessed — main contractor representative unavailable". An inspection certificate signed by a sub-contractor's own site QC, in the absence of the Contractor, is not a contractually effective certification under the Sub-Clause equivalent in the Kallio subcontract.
- **The signing date is two days after the claimed completion date.** A self-certification signed 20.09.2026 for work claimed to be complete on 18.09.2026 is not a contemporaneous record of the completion event.
- **The certificate is contradicted by the QS-measured state of the work.** The Contractor's QS walked the slab on 28–30.09.2026 and found the W4 upstands incomplete. The certification was therefore either inaccurate as to the state of the work at the time of certification, or the work was allowed to deteriorate between certification and the QS walk. Either way, the certificate does not represent the as-built state.
- **The bay W4 upstands are not a minor or ambiguous residual.** They are a 24 m continuous section of the most critical waterproofing termination on the slab. The QS cannot certify the waterproofing as complete while the upstand is not formed.

The certificate is also undermined by the **company name change** in the P07 returns — Kallio & Maa Oy (the name on the Kallio subcontract and on the earlier KMO-QC-0207) has been replaced by Kallio Maanrakennus Oy on the P07 return. The Contractor has not yet been notified of any change to the subcontract parties; the named signing party on KMO-QC-0219 is to be confirmed.

## 4. The consequence

DH2 ground-floor slab **pour 1 of 4 was placed on 21 September 2026 over the affected area**. The Contractor records that the slab was placed on the basis of the Kallio 100% certification. The consequence of the non-conforming work having been buried by the slab is:

- **The defect cannot be visually inspected without removing the placed slab.** Removal would be a destructive and costly remediation.
- **The waterproofing integrity at the upstand is uncertain as built.** The membrane is present but the termination of the membrane to the upstand is not formed; the protection layer is present but unsupported at the upstand location; the design assumption of a continuous sealed upstand is not met.
- **Until the W4 upstands are completed and re-inspected, the waterproofing integrity of the area buried by pour 1 cannot be confirmed.** The performance of the substructure in service is contingent on the integrity of the waterproofing system.
- **DH2 slab pours 2, 3 and 4 are contingent on the upstands in adjacent bays being completed to standard.** The Contractor will not issue further pour releases until the W4 upstands are completed and the bays adjacent to the further pours are confirmed compliant.

The Contractor's QS has reduced the percentage cum for C-1180 to 90% for the purposes of the Period 07 progress report (P07 cycle 2) and the cost-value reconciliation (PDC-CVR-P07). The 10% withheld (EUR 21,000 of EUR 210,000) will be released on completion of the W4 upstand works and re-inspection by the Contractor.

## 5. What is required to close this NCR

TP-001 Kallio is required to:

1. **Complete the W4 upstands — approximately 24 m of perimeter upstand at the north edge of bay W4** — to the standard detailed in the contract drawings and the Kallio method statement. **Due 07 October 2026.**

2. **Re-present the affected area for inspection by the Contractor.** Inspection request to be raised as KMO-QC-0220 or equivalent, referencing this NCR (PDC-NCR-001). **Due 09 October 2026 at the latest.** All future Kallio inspection requests on this scope must be witnessed by the Contractor (the witness must sign in the appropriate box on the certificate).

3. **Provide a written method statement for the assessment of the as-built waterproofing integrity in the slab pour 1 area adjacent to the affected length**, including (a) review of photograph records of the pour 1 area on 21.09.2026, (b) review of the membrane termination details against the design, (c) a statement of conformance or non-conformance, and (d) any compensatory measures proposed. **Due 14 October 2026.**

4. **Refrain from further pours of DH2 slab (pours 2, 3, 4) until the Contractor has issued a pour release for each.** No further pour will be authorised until the W4 upstands are completed and the bays adjacent to the next pour are confirmed compliant.

5. **Provide a written acknowledgement of this NCR within 5 working days** of receipt, including a programme for the corrective works and the name of the Kallio engineer responsible for the closure.

6. **Confirm the named signing party on KMO-QC-0219** and the current named party on the Kallio subcontract. The Certificate Register in the Companion Workbook records this as an open item.

### Notes on the draft (for the package manager — not for transmission)

1. **Keep it factual.** The user instruction was: "what was certified, what was measured, what the consequence is, what is required to close". The draft does that. The commercial position is in §6 — not in the body of the NCR — because the NCR is a quality document, not a commercial one.

2. **The 24 m number is approximate.** The QS measure was a walk on 28–30.09 with linear measurement; ±2 m is reasonable. State "approximately 24 m" in the issue, do not round to 25 m.

3. **The clause references are deliberately thin.** This is a Kallio package NCR, not a main-contract claim. Cite the Kallio subcontract and the contract drawings; do not import FIDIC Yellow Book clauses unless the package subcontract references them.

4. **The slab poured over the non-conforming work is a separate fact.** It is recorded in §4 (the consequence) and in the Period 07 report §4. Kallio will likely push back on the linkage. The response is the photographs from 21.09.2026 and the QS walks of 28–30.09.2026. Hold the line on the factual record.

5. **The 10% withholding is the leverage.** C-1180 value is EUR 210,000. 10% = EUR 21,000. That is roughly the cost of the upstand rectification in day-work terms. Recovery is a separate matter and is a commercial, not a quality, decision.

6. **KMO-QC-0219 was unwitnessed — this is the smoking gun.** It is the basis on which the certification is challenged in §3. The Certificate Register sheet in the companion workbook records this as the operative fact. Kallio's own record on the P07 progress return says "not witnessed — main contractor representative unavailable"; we cite their own record against them.
