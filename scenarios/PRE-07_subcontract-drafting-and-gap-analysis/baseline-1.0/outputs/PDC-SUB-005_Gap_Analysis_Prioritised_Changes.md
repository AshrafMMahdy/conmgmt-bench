# PDC-SUB-005 Rev 0 — Ilmatek TP-005 Subcontract: Prioritised Changes Required

**Review date:** 3 August 2026  
**Subcontract sum:** EUR 22,286,880 (excl. VAT)  
**Subcontractor:** Ilmatek LVI Oy  
**Reviewed against:** Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C (FIDIC Yellow Book 2017 Particular Conditions) · Sectional Completion Schedule PDC-SC-001 · Tender Offer IL-7729 (19 March 2026)

---

## Executive Summary

Ilmatek's subcontract draft is **not fit to sign** in its current form. It contains **17 material deviations** — 5 CRITICAL, 8 HIGH, and 4 MEDIUM — from the main contract and their own tender. The subcontract adopts Swedish law and Stockholm arbitration for a Finnish project under a Finnish-law main contract, gives the subcontractor a unilateral right to suspend variation work, grants full extensions of time for concurrent delay (the main contract apportions to zero), halves the defects period, doubles retention, and caps liability at 50%. All of these must be fixed before execution.

Critically, they tendered on YSE 1998 but have submitted their own standard conditions. The document structure is Part 1 (Particulars) + Part 2 (Conditions, Clauses 1–13) + Appendix A (Scope Schedule) + Signature block — all present and reviewed.

## CRITICAL — Must be resolved before signature (5 items)

### C1. Governing law must be Finland, not Sweden
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 13.1 / Part 1 (Governing Law) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Sub-Clause 1.4 (Governing Law) |
| **Subcontract says** | Swedish law |
| **Main contract says** | Finnish law |
| **Risk** | Different limitation periods, different contract interpretation rules, different mandatory protections. The Contractor would have obligations to the Employer under Finnish law but remedies against the Subcontractor under Swedish law — an irreconcilable back-to-back failure. Both the Subcontractor (Ilmatek LVI Oy) and the project (Vantaa) are Finnish. |
| **Required change** | Replace "Sweden" with "Finland". |

### C2. Dispute resolution must mirror the main contract — Helsinki, three arbitrators, FCC Rules
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 13.2 / Part 1 (Dispute Resolution) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Sub-Clause 21.6 |
| **Subcontract says** | SCC Rules, Stockholm, one arbitrator, English |
| **Main contract says** | Finland Chamber of Commerce Rules, Helsinki, three arbitrators, English |
| **Risk** | Parallel dispute-resolution tracks with different seats make joined proceedings impossible. Inconsistent awards are a real possibility — the Contractor could lose against the Employer in Helsinki while winning against the Subcontractor in Stockholm on the same facts. One arbitrator for a EUR 22.3M dispute is inadequate. |
| **Required change** | Replace with: "Arbitration under the Rules of the Arbitration Institute of the Finland Chamber of Commerce, seat Helsinki, three arbitrators, language English." |

### C3. Concurrent delay must adopt apportionment, not full EOT
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 4.2 |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-7 |
| **Subcontract says** | Full extension of time for the whole period of concurrency even where the Subcontractor is also at fault |
| **Main contract says** | Apportionment — no extension of time or cost entitlement for periods of concurrent delay (FIDIC Sub-Clause 8.5 modified) |
| **Risk** | The Contractor would be liable to the Subcontractor for time it cannot recover from the Employer. This is a direct financial exposure that the main contract does not allow to be passed up. |
| **Required change** | Delete the entire clause. Replace with: *"Where a delay for which the Subcontractor is responsible occurs concurrently with a delay entitling the Subcontractor to an extension of time, the Subcontractor's entitlement to an extension of time shall be apportioned such that the Subcontractor receives no extension of time for the period of concurrency, consistent with Sub-Clause 8.5 of the main contract."* |

### C4. Delete the variation suspension right — subcontractor must proceed first
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 7.3 |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-9 |
| **Subcontract says** | Subcontractor may suspend variation work if value is not agreed within 14 days, without liability for consequent delay |
| **Main contract says** | Contractor shall proceed with any variation upon instruction; Engineer makes an interim valuation within 28 days |
| **Risk** | This gives the Subcontractor a coercive tool the Contractor has no equivalent of. If the Subcontractor suspends, the overall programme is disrupted and the Contractor has no recourse. Under the Finnish Unfair Contract Terms Act (1255/1993), this clause is likely unenforceable. |
| **Required change** | Delete the clause. Replace with: *"The Subcontractor shall proceed with any instructed variation without delay. Where the value is not agreed within 14 days, the Contractor shall make an interim valuation and the Subcontractor shall be paid the interim amount within the Period for Payment. The interim valuation is without prejudice to the final agreed value."* |

### C5. Subcontract form must be YSE 1998, as tendered
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Part 1 — "Form of subcontract" |
| **Conflicting document** | Tender Offer IL-7729 (19 March 2026) — "YSE 1998, subject to section 4 below" |
| **Subcontract says** | Subcontractor's own standard conditions (Part 2, Clauses 1–13) |
| **Tender offered** | YSE 1998 (Rakennusurakan yleiset sopimusehdot — the Finnish General Conditions for Building Contracts) |
| **Risk** | This is a fundamental departure from the basis on which the tender was evaluated. YSE 1998 is the known, understood, industry-standard framework in Finland. The Subcontractor's own conditions — while present and reviewed in full — are significantly less favourable than YSE 1998. This alone is grounds to reject the draft. |
| **Required change** | The subcontract must be on YSE 1998 terms as tendered, with any bespoke amendments set out transparently. Alternatively, a full FIDIC-based back-to-back subcontract (consistent with the main contract) may be acceptable — but not the Subcontractor's own form. |

## HIGH — Must be resolved (8 items)

### H1. Add sectional completion obligations with per-section LD rates
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 3.3 |
| **Conflicting document** | Sectional Completion Schedule PDC-SC-001 |
| **Subcontract says** | Single completion obligation; "no sectional or partial completion dates, obligations or liabilities" unless stated in Part 1 |
| **Main contract says** | Four sections: Section 1 (2 May 2027, EUR 18K/day), Section 2 (2 Jul 2027, EUR 12K/day), Section 3 (2 Oct 2027, EUR 12K/day), Section 4 (2 Jan 2028, EUR 10K/day) |
| **Risk** | The subcontract's single completion date (6 March 2027) falls before Section 1 — which is chronologically achievable — but there is no mechanism if the Subcontractor's work becomes critical-path to sectional handovers. The single EUR 15,000/day LD rate does not match the main contract structure. |
| **Required change** | Insert sectional milestones matching Sections 1 and 2 in Part 1, with LD rates that mirror the main contract. |

### H2. Cap liquidated damages at 10% of the Subcontract Sum
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 5.2 / Part 1 (Aggregate limit: "None") |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-13 |
| **Subcontract says** | EUR 15,000 per day, no aggregate limit |
| **Main contract says** | Maximum Delay Damages = 10% of Accepted Contract Amount |
| **Risk** | Unlimited LD exposure on the subcontract while the Contractor's own liability is capped at 10%. Under Finnish Contracts Act Section 36, uncapped LDs may be adjusted as unreasonable. |
| **Required change** | Insert: *"The aggregate of delay damages payable by the Subcontractor shall not exceed 10% of the Subcontract Sum."* |

### H3. Reduce retention to 5%, release half at Section 1
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 6.2 / Part 1 (Retention) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-12 |
| **Subcontract says** | 10% retention on each payment, released in full on completion |
| **Main contract says** | 5% retention; one half released on completion of Section 1, remainder at end of DNP |
| **Risk** | Double the retention and held entirely until the end. EUR 2.23M cash trapped instead of EUR 1.11M, and no early release. The Contractor cannot pass this cost up. |
| **Required change** | Reduce to 5%; release 50% on completion of Section 1 and 50% on expiry of DNP. |

### H4. Extend defects liability period to 24 months
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 8.1 / Part 1 (Defects Liability Period) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Sub-Clause 11.1 |
| **Subcontract says** | 12 months from completion |
| **Main contract says** | 24 months (DNP) |
| **Risk** | The Contractor would carry DLP exposure for the mechanical works for an additional 12 months with no subcontractor obligation to return and remedy. If a defect emerges at month 18, the Contractor pays to fix it with no right of recovery from Ilmatek. |
| **Required change** | Replace "12 months" with "24 months". |

### H5. Increase professional indemnity insurance to EUR 10M for 6 years
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 9.3 / Part 1 (PI Insurance) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-16 |
| **Subcontract says** | EUR 2,000,000 each and every claim, maintained for 3 years from completion |
| **Main contract says** | EUR 10,000,000, maintained for 6 years |
| **Risk** | The Contractor carries an EUR 8M coverage gap for 3–6 years post-completion. A design defect in the mechanical system could give rise to a claim that exhausts the subcontractor's cover, leaving the Contractor with the balance. |
| **Required change** | Increase to EUR 10,000,000 for 6 years. |

### H6. Remove or substantially increase the 50% liability cap
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 9.1 / Part 1 (Limit of Liability) |
| **Conflicting document** | Tender IL-7729 (implied 100% / Subcontract Sum) |
| **Subcontract says** | Total liability capped at 50% of the Subcontract Sum |
| **Tender offered** | No cap stated — market-standard expectation of full liability up to the Subcontract Sum |
| **Risk** | For any loss over EUR 11.14M, the Contractor has zero additional recovery. This walked back from the tender. The Finnish Unfair Contract Terms Act may treat this cap as unreasonable given the design obligations. |
| **Required change** | Remove the cap entirely, or set it at no less than 100% of the Subcontract Sum. At minimum, carve out gross negligence, wilful misconduct, and breach of design obligations. |

### H7. Performance security should be no less than 10% of the Subcontract Sum
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 10.1 / Part 1 (Performance Security) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Sub-Clause 4.2 |
| **Subcontract says** | 5% |
| **Main contract says** | 10% of Accepted Contract Amount (required from Contractor to Employer) |
| **Risk** | If the Contractor is required to provide 10% to the Employer but only holds 5% from the Subcontractor, the Contractor carries the balance of the exposure — EUR 1.11M uncovered for this package alone. |
| **Required change** | Increase to 10% of the Subcontract Sum. |

### H8. Design obligation must include guaranteed performance outcomes
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 2.3 |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-6 |
| **Subcontract says** | "Reasonable skill and care" |
| **Main contract says** | Reasonable skill and care PLUS guaranteed performance outcomes — PUE, WUE ≤ 0.25 L/kWh, free cooling ≥ 8,300 h/yr, waste-heat delivery ≥ 4 MW at ≥ 70°C |
| **Risk** | The general design obligation is a professional-negligence standard while the main contract requires guaranteed outcomes. The Subcontractor's Clause 2.2 does list performance criteria, but the overarching design obligation may not hold them to those criteria as guarantees. |
| **Required change** | Amend to: *"The Subcontractor shall exercise reasonable skill and care in the design and shall achieve the performance outcomes set out in Clause 2.2, which are guaranteed."* |

## MEDIUM — Should be resolved (4 items)

### M1. Align payment period with main contract SP-4(e) and tender offer
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 6.1 / Part 1 (Period for Payment) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Condition SP-4(e) / Tender IL-7729 |
| **Subcontract says** | 60 days from receipt of invoice |
| **Main contract says** | Contractor to use reasonable endeavours to ensure subcontractors are paid no earlier than 30 days — with the Employer paying 45 days after IPC |
| **Risk** | The cashflow gap: the Contractor pays the Subcontractor at day 60 but cannot recover from the Employer until ~day 73. For each monthly payment (~EUR 1.86M), the Contractor carries ~13 working days of float. Tender IL-7729 offered 45 days — the subcontract has walked this back to 60. |
| **Required change** | Reduce to 45 days as tendered, or at minimum 30 days to comply with SP-4(e), with pay-when-paid protection. |

### M2. Late payment interest at Finnish statutory rate
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Clause 6.3 |
| **Conflicting document** | Finnish Interest Act (633/1982) |
| **Subcontract says** | 8% per annum |
| **Finnish statutory rate** | ECB reference rate + 8 percentage points (currently ~12% per annum) |
| **Risk** | A flat 8% may fall below the statutory minimum for commercial contracts, potentially rendering the clause unenforceable. |
| **Required change** | Amend to: *"Interest at the rate prescribed by the Finnish Interest Act (633/1982) for commercial contracts."* |

### M3. Commencement should reference the sectional programme
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Part 1 (Date for Commencement) |
| **Conflicting document** | Sectional Completion Schedule PDC-SC-001 |
| **Subcontract says** | Date for Commencement: within 10 working days of instruction |
| **Main contract schedule** | Commencement Date: 2 March 2026 |
| **Risk** | If acceptance occurs now (August 2026), commencement would be October 2026 — 7 months after main contract commencement. This compresses the programme or pushes the single completion date beyond Section 1. The subcontract completion date (6 March 2027) was based on the original tender programme, not on a floating start. |
| **Required change** | Fix the commencement date to align with the main contract programme, and adjust the Time for Completion accordingly. |

### M4. Advance payment terms are acceptable — maintain
| | |
|---|---|
| **Subcontract ref** | Part 1 (Advance Payment) |
| **Conflicting document** | Main Contract PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Sub-Clause 14.2 |
| **Assessment** | Advance payment of 10%, repaid from each IPC at 12% of the amount otherwise due — these terms match the main contract. No change required. |
