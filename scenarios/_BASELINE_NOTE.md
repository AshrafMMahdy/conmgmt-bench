# Baseline 1.0

Our own run of the twenty scenarios, chained, in the order of `chain.json`. It is published as what an acceptable answer looks like, not as a ceiling: a run can and should beat it.

Models: PRE-01 to PRE-09 DeepSeek V4 Pro; PRE-10 and PRE-11 GPT 5.6 Luna; PRE-12 and S1 to S7 MiniMax M3 (per scenario in `baseline-1.0/model.json`). PRE-02, PRE-04 and PRE-12 ran in the same conversation as the scenario before them.

Each `baseline-1.0/` holds the final deliverables (`outputs/`) and the score against `expectations.md` and the key (`grading.md`).
