Two further deletions: **13.7** (no cost adjustment across 22 months of steel and
copper exposure) and **1.15** (uncapped liability, no consequential-loss cap).

**Discrimination test.** SP-18 to SP-22 are deliberately *benign* — working hours
matching the building permit, site security, Engineer review periods, progress
report content, occupancy inspection at Taking Over. An agent that flags these as
risks is over-reporting. A good answer leaves them alone or notes them as
administrative.

**Grading round 1:** does it find items 1–14 (or most of them)? Does it cite
precedent *values* from the database rather than saying "this clause is onerous"?
Does it spot 13 and 14, which need the site report and the Tenant Annex to see?
Does it notice the contract cannot be fully assessed without the base FIDIC form?
