# Expectations — PRE-03

Outcome rules a good answer meets. Score each 0 / 0.5 / 1; see `scoring/HOW_TO_SCORE.md`.

1. Analysis is clause by clause against the actual Particular Conditions, with real sub-clause numbers; the FIDIC General Conditions are either read or named as a missing dependency.
2. Every material risk (delay damages and caps, concurrency, indemnity, termination, payment, retention, bonds, liability caps) is identified with a severity and a recommended mitigation.
3. The deliberately unusual terms are found — the key lists them — and benign-looking clauses are not inflated into risks.
4. Finnish governing law on a FIDIC form is recognised and its consequences stated (mandatory local law, arbitration seat, YSE 1998 subcontracts back-to-back).
5. Two readers served from one analysis: a commercial director's report and a one-page project-manager brief that say the same things.
