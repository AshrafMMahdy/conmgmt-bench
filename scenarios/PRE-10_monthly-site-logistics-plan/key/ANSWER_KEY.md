# Key — PRE-10 monthly site logistics plan

- One plan per month for the WHOLE construction period: Month 1 (Mar 2026) to Month 22 (Dec 2027), external works included — not stopping at Jun 2027.
- Sizing computed from the company standard (PDC-LOG-001 §5), not guessed: e.g. 55 people ÷ 2.5 = 22 parking spaces × 25 m² = 550 m², ramping with headcount.
- Per-subcontractor storage compounds named, and relocating as the fronts move.
- Crane positions with radii; the 110 kV / DNO exclusion from PDC-DWG-001 respected and called out as a constraint drawing cannot fix.
- Pedestrian routes separated from the vehicle route and gate.
- Deliverables as asked: a deck (one marked-up plan per month), a spreadsheet, a PM covering note — and if any of the three is not delivered, the answer says so.
