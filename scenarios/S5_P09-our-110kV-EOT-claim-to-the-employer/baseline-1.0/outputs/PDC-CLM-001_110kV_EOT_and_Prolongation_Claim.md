## 1. Executive summary

The Employer revised the 110 kV grid energisation date by 43 calendar days / 30 working days. The activity most affected is integrated systems testing on Level 4 (ST-1310), which is on the critical path, dependent on the Employer-provided EN-110 milestone and on the Ilmatek TP-005 mechanical commissioning activity (IL-1600) immediately upstream of it.

The Contractor's claim is for **21 working days of extension of the Time for Completion**, and for **EUR 112,746 of Cost**. The Employer-attributable share of the slip is 30 working days under the records; 9 working days are concurrent Contractor-side delay on the L4 structural handover (ST-1300), attributable to TP-001 Kallio's rebar revision and revised pour sequence. The 30/9 apportionment is proposed under Rev C Sub-Clause SP-7; the Contractor does not claim Cost on the concurrent 9 wd, in line with that clause.

Subcontractor TP-005 Ilmatek has reserved its position on the same event under Subcontract PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1 Clause 1.3. The Contractor's assessment (PDC-LTR-005-011 of 13.11.2026) records this as a reservation, with quantum to be assessed and notified separately under Subcontract procedure. The Contractor has settled separately with Ilmatek on Head D (TC-02 tower-crane outage, EUR 12,400), which is a Contractor-risk item and is **not** part of this claim.

## 2. Head 1 — Validity of PDC-NOT-009 (Rev C Sub-Clause 20.2.2)

### 2.1 The Contractor's position

The Engineer's letter PDC-LTR-PMC-021 (28.10.2026), paragraph 3, asserts that PDC-NOT-009 (received 9 October 2026) was given **42 days** after the Contractor became aware of the event on 28 August 2026 (PDC-LTR-PMC-014), and is therefore barred under Sub-Clause 20.2.1.

The Contractor does not contest the 42-day figure. The Contractor contends that, on the Contract that governs, the bar is not settled by the Contractor's date alone. Under Rev C SP-15, both Sub-Clauses 20.2.1 and 20.2.2 are reinstated in full. Sub-Clause 20.2.2 imposes a 14-day window on the Engineer, on receipt of a Notice of Claim, to give notice of non-compliance with reasons. If the Engineer does not give such a notice within 14 days, the Notice is **deemed to be a valid Notice**.

### 2.2 Verbatim wording relied upon

**Rev C SP-15** — operative text reproduced verbatim in PDC-CX-009, *Sub-Clause 20.2 Operative Text* (25.11.2026), paragraphs 1–2. PDC-CX-009 is the operative-text file note authored by SBI Commercial for use in any notice-position assessment under the executed Rev C Particular Conditions. The note attributes the wording to SP-15 of PDC-PC-001 Rev C:

> "Rev C governs. SP-15 withdraws the Rev A and Rev B amendments, reinstates Sub-Clause 20.2.1 in full and unamended with the 28-day period, and applies Sub-Clause 20.2.2 in full, including the Engineer's obligation to give notice of non-compliance and the consequences of failing to do so."

**Sub-Clause 20.2.2 second paragraph** — operative text reproduced verbatim in PDC-CX-009, paragraphs 3–4, attributed to PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Particular Conditions, SP-15:

> "If the Engineer considers that the Contractor has failed to give the Notice of Claim within the period of 28 days, the Engineer shall, within 14 days after receiving the Notice of Claim, give a Notice to the Contractor accordingly, with reasons.
>
> If the Engineer does not give such a Notice within that period of 14 days, the Notice of Claim shall be deemed to be a valid Notice. If the Contractor disagrees with a Notice given by the Engineer under this Sub-Clause, or considers there are circumstances which justify the late submission, the Contractor shall include the details in the fully detailed Claim."

**Source for the two quotations above: PDC-CX-009** (operative-text file note, 25.11.2026). PDC-PC-001 Rev C is the contract that contains SP-15; CX-009 reproduces the operative wording verbatim for the purposes of this and any future notice-position assessment.

### 2.3 Application to PDC-NOT-009

| Event | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Contractor aware of event (PMC-014) | 28.08.2026 | PDC-LTR-PMC-014, paragraph 1 |
| Notice of Claim served (NOT-009) | 09.10.2026 | PDC-NOT-009, header; PMC-021, paragraph 1 |
| Engineer non-compliance letter (PMC-021) | 28.10.2026 | PDC-LTR-PMC-021, header |
| Days from Notice to Engineer response | 19 days | arithmetic |
| Window allowed to Engineer under 20.2.2 | 14 days | PDC-CX-009 paragraphs 3–4 (Sub-Clause 20.2.2) |
| Engineer response | **5 days late** | 19 − 14 |

The Engineer's PDC-LTR-PMC-021 was therefore not given within the 14-day window stipulated in Sub-Clause 20.2.2. Under the second paragraph of Sub-Clause 20.2.2, PDC-NOT-009 is **deemed to be a valid Notice**.

### 2.4 Reservation on the 20.2.1 wording

Without prejudice to the 20.2.2 deemed-validity route above, the Contractor records for completeness that under Rev C Sub-Clause 20.2.1 the Notice was given "as soon as practicable" within the meaning of that phrase. The 42-day period between 28.08.2026 and 09.10.2026 reflects the time taken to:

- Receive and log PMC-014 (received 28.08.2026);
- Receive Vantaan Energia Oy's confirmation of the regional outage reprogramming (logged 04.09.2026);
- Re-cost the preliminaries impact from the priced BOQ Section A and reconcile against the schedule float (week 36);
- Take legal advice on the operative notice provisions under the executed Rev C of the Particular Conditions (week 37);
- Draft and clear the Notice through SBI Commercial (week 40).

These steps were sequential and unavoidable. The Contractor reserves the right to expand on them if the Engineer disputes the "as soon as practicable" position despite the 20.2.2 outcome.

### 3.3 Critical-path causation

The Contractor's current working programme, period 09 update (PDC-PROG-P09, sheet "Critical Path — Nov 2026") records the following logic and float on the controlling activities:

| Activity | Description | Duration | Predecessor | Total float | Critical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ST-1300 | L4 structural handover to MEP (TP-001 Kallio) | 30 wd | ST-1210 FS | 0 | Yes |
| IL-1600 | Mechanical commissioning, DH1–DH4 (Ilmatek) | 45 wd | ST-1300 FS | 0 | Yes |
| ST-1310 | Integrated systems testing, L4 | 35 wd | IL-1600 FS, **EN-110 FS** | 0 | Yes |
| EN-110 | 110 kV grid energisation (Employer-provided) | 0 wd | — | 0 | Yes |
| ST-1400 | Client witness testing and handover | 20 wd | ST-1310 FS | 0 | Yes |

The sheet's note states: *"ST-1310 cannot commence before EN-110. Energisation is Employer-provided under the Contract."*

ST-1310 cannot start before **both** IL-1600 (the Ilmatek upstream activity) and EN-110 (the Employer-provided milestone) have finished. EN-110 is Employer-provided and the revised date is firm. The 30 wd slip on EN-110 propagates directly through ST-1310 to ST-1400 and to the Time for Completion.

### 3.4 Apportionment of the slip — Rev C Sub-Clause SP-7

The Contractor's own site records (PDC-PROG-P09, sheet "Concurrent Delay Record", ref C-01) record a 9-wd slip on ST-1300 (L4 structural handover) — planned 15.02.2027, forecast 26.02.2027 — attributable to TP-001 Kallio's rebar revision and revised pour sequence. This slip is at the Contractor's risk and is independent of the energisation event. The sheet notes: *"9 of the 30 working days are therefore concurrent."*

**Rev C SP-7** (Particular Conditions, PDC-PC-001 Rev C, Part B Special Provisions):

> "Where a delay for which the Contractor is responsible is concurrent with a delay for which the Contractor would otherwise be entitled to an extension of time, the Engineer shall apportion the delay between the concurrent causes on a fair and reasonable basis, having regard to the contemporary records. The Contractor shall be entitled to an extension of time for the apportioned share attributable to the Employer's risk events, and shall not be entitled to Cost for any period of concurrent delay."

The Contractor proposes the apportionment itself, under the contemporaneous record:

- 30 wd of slip on the energisation chain (Employer risk);
- 9 wd of slip on ST-1300 L4 handover (Contractor risk, recorded in "Concurrent Delay Record");
- **Apportioned EOT to the Contractor: 21 wd**, being the Employer's share of 30 wd less the 9 wd of Contractor-side concurrent slip.

The Contractor invites the Engineer to confirm the apportionment, or to propose a different fair-and-reasonable basis under SP-7 if the Engineer takes a different view on the contemporary records. The Contractor does not seek EOT on the 9 wd of its own slip.

### 3.5 Note on PDC-NOT-009

The Notice PDC-NOT-009 (9.10.2026), paragraph 1, records the contract date as "2 March 2027". This was an internal dating error in the Notice; the Employer's letter PDC-LTR-PMC-014 of 28.08.2026, paragraph 1, records the contract date as **12 March 2027**, and the corrected period 9 progress sheet PDC-PROG-P09 names PDC-LTR-PMC-014 as the source of the date. The Notice's slip figure, the claim's slip figure and the period 9 progress sheet's slip figure are now all consistent with 12 March 2027. The Notice's internal dating error does not affect Head 1 (validity under 20.2.2), nor Head 2 (the slip is computed from the corrected records).

### 3.6 EOT sought

The Contractor claims an extension of the Time for Completion under Sub-Clause 20.2 of **21 working days** (4.2 calendar weeks).

## 7. Head 6 — Reserved pass-through: Ilmatek TP-005, Head B (110 kV revised energisation)

This is a **reservation** under Subcontract PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1, not a claim quantum.

### 7.1 Subcontract wording relied upon

**PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1, Clause 1.3**:

> "The Subcontractor's rights and obligations in relation to the Subcontract Works are co-extensive with the Contractor's rights and obligations under the Main Contract, so far as they apply to those works. The Subcontractor shall not be entitled to time or money in circumstances where the Contractor would not be entitled to the equivalent relief from the Employer, and no entitlement arises unless and to the extent the Contractor recovers from the Employer in respect of the same matter."

### 7.2 Application

Ilmatek is the Subcontractor for the mechanical commissioning scope (activity IL-1600), which is the immediate upstream predecessor of ST-1310 (integrated systems testing). The 110 kV revised energisation event on PDC-LTR-PMC-014 of 28.08.2026 is a Main Contract Employer-risk event affecting the same critical-path activity (ST-1310) that the Ilmatek activity (IL-1600) drives into.

Ilmatek's Notice ILM-NOT-004 of 02.10.2026 notified Head B (14 working days, original quantum EUR 96,000, subsequently Q2 EUR 31,360 in v6) within the 21-day Subcontract Clause 4.3 bar. The Contractor's assessment PDC-LTR-005-011 of 13.11.2026 recorded Ilmatek's Head B as **"0 wd now, reserved"**, with the bearing-on-this-claim note: *"Same 110 kV event as this claim. Their entitlement is bound to ours under Subcontract Clause 1.3 — it follows what we recover, and follows nothing if we recover nothing."*

The Contractor notifies the Employer under this Head 6 that the Ilmatek entitlement is contemplated on this Main Contract claim and will follow what the Contractor recovers under Heads 2 and 3 above, in accordance with Subcontract Clause 1.3.

**Quantum is not stated.** Ilmatek's quantum on Head B will be assessed on the Subcontract records (gang sizes, lost-time, plant logs) and settled via the Subcontract variation and certification procedure. The Employer is not asked at this stage to value or assess the Ilmatek entitlement — only to note that it is co-extensive with the Contractor's recovery under Subcontract Clause 1.3.

### 7.3 A note on the parallel notice regimes

PDC-CX-009 (25.11.2026) records that *"the subcontract PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1 Clause 4.3 has no equivalent of 20.2.2. Its 21-day bar is absolute and operates on the subcontractor's date alone."* The Contractor draws the distinction between the two regimes so that the Employer understands the back-to-back is on quantum, not on notice.

This is **not** a claim head. It is a named-and-excluded line.

TC-02 tower crane was out of service 05–07.10.2026. Ilmatek's pipefitting and welding gang was stood down during the outage. The standing-time cost to Ilmatek is **EUR 12,400**.

### 8.2 Attribution

The Contractor's plant log (PDC-LAB-P08) records TC-02 outage responsibility as: *"the outage was the Contractor's own maintenance and is accepted as the Contractor's risk."* The Contractor's letter PDC-LTR-005-009 of 06.10.2026 confirmed the outage to Ilmatek without denial of liability. Notice ILM-NOT-005 of 08.10.2026 is within Subcontract Clause 4.3. The EUR 12,400 was accepted in the Contractor's assessment PDC-LTR-005-011 of 13.11.2026, line: *"EUR 12,400 accepted — TC-02 is our own tower crane and the outage is recorded in our plant log as our own maintenance. This sum is ours to bear."*

### 8.3 Settlement

The EUR 12,400 is being certified in the November valuation under action A09-03 of Meeting No. 9 (24.11.2026): *"Certify EUR 12,400 to Ilmatek in the November valuation | MN | 05.12.2026"*.

### 8.4 Statement of exclusion

**The EUR 12,400 is NOT included in the EUR 18,400 cost-incurred head (Head 4) and is NOT included in the EUR 112,746 total claim.** It is a Contractor-risk cost borne under Subcontract PDC-SUB-005 Rev 1 and recovered through the Ilmatek valuation; it is not a Main Contract Employer-recoverable item.

The Contractor names this figure here so that:

- it is not inadvertently claimed twice (once paid to Ilmatek under the November valuation, once recovered from the Employer under Head 4);
- it is locatable at year-end reconciliation as a Subcontract item, not a Main Contract item;
- the Employer has the same picture the Contractor has.

## 10. What is not claimed

In line with the Contractor's instructions of 26.11.2026 (PDC-CLM-001-plan-002):

- No disruption quantum. Productivity / stacking evidence for IL-1600 and ST-1310 is reserved for a later submission (Head 5).
- No financing charges under Sub-Clause 14.8 / Rev C SP-12. No certified IPC supports a financing-charge calculation at this stage.

FIDIC Yellow Book 2017 Sub-Clause 20.2.6 entitles "Cost" as defined in Sub-Clause 1.1.7 — reasonable cost actually incurred or to be incurred. Head office absorption and profit-on-cost do not meet that definition on a prolongation claim under Yellow Book.
- No claim on the 9 wd of Contractor-side L4 handover slip.
- No acceleration claim. The records do not show acceleration.
- No Tenancy-side or service-credit pass-through (not the Contractor's exposure under the Main Contract).
