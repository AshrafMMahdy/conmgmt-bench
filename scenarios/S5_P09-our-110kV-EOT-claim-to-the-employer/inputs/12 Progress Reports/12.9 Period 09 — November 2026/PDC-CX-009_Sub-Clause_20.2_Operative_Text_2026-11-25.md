## Why this note exists

We have been working to the Rev B wording on notices in at least one earlier report. **Rev C
governs.** SP-15 withdraws the Rev A and Rev B amendments, reinstates Sub-Clause 20.2.1 in full and
unamended **with the 28-day period**, and applies **Sub-Clause 20.2.2 in full**, including the
Engineer's obligation to give notice of non-compliance and the consequences of failing to do so.

## Sub-Clause 20.2.1 — Notice of Claim (operative text)

> "If the Contractor considers itself to be entitled to any additional payment or an extension of
> the Time for Completion, the Contractor shall give a Notice to the Engineer describing the event
> or circumstance giving rise to the claim. **The Notice shall be given as soon as practicable, and
> not later than 28 days after the Contractor became aware, or should have become aware, of the
> event or circumstance.**
>
> If the Contractor fails to give a Notice of Claim within this period of 28 days, the Contractor
> shall not be entitled to any additional payment, and the Time for Completion shall not be
> extended, in respect of that event or circumstance."

## Sub-Clause 20.2.2 — Engineer's initial response (operative text)

> "**If the Engineer considers that the Contractor has failed to give the Notice of Claim within the
> period of 28 days, the Engineer shall, within 14 days after receiving the Notice of Claim, give a
> Notice to the Contractor accordingly, with reasons.**
>
> **If the Engineer does not give such a Notice within that period of 14 days, the Notice of Claim
> shall be deemed to be a valid Notice.** If the Contractor disagrees with a Notice given by the
> Engineer under this Sub-Clause, or considers there are circumstances which justify the late
> submission, the Contractor shall include the details in the fully detailed Claim."

## What this means in practice

Two clocks run, not one, and the second belongs to the Engineer. Whether a late Notice is barred is
not settled by the Contractor's date alone. Anyone assessing a notice position under this Contract —
ours or a subcontractor's — has to check both.

Note for completeness: the subcontract PDC-SUB-005 Rev1 Clause 4.3 has **no equivalent of 20.2.2**.
Its 21-day bar is absolute and operates on the subcontractor's date alone. The two regimes are not
the same and should not be reasoned about as if they were.
