# Expectations — PRE-12

Outcome rules a good answer meets. Score each 0 / 0.5 / 1; see `scoring/HOW_TO_SCORE.md`.

1. Each reported defect is verified against the workbook before being accepted — including the one the user got partly wrong (the sign of peak funding).
2. Corrections are made in place in every document that carries the figure, not by rebuilding the pack.
3. After correction, all headline figures reconcile across the three documents and the workbook formula reproduces the stated minimum.
4. The answer states plainly what was wrong, what changed, and what was re-checked.
