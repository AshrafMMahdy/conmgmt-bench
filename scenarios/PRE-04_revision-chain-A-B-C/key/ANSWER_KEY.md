# Key — PRE-04 Rev B and Rev C

## 2. CONTRACT ANALYSIS — ROUND 2 (Rev B)

Rev B contains a concession matrix listing what the Employer conceded and refused.
**It does not mention what the Employer added.** That omission is the test.

**Conceded fully:** payment 75→45 days, certification 42→28 days, DNP 36→24
months, arbitration moved to FCC Helsinki, pay-when-paid withdrawn.

**Conceded partially — still worse than precedent:**

| Item | Rev B | Precedent |
|---|---|---|
| LD cap | 12% | 10% |
| Retention | 7.5%, bond only after Section 1, release still deferred | 5%, cash-or-bond, released at Taking Over |
| Advance payment | 5% | 8–10% |
| Subcontractor payment | 45 days | 30 days |
| Claim notice | 21 days, still absolute, still no waiver | 28 days + 20.2.2 route |
| 4.12 | reinstated but **time only, no cost**, and the site report's residual risks **deemed foreseeable** | full 4.12 relief |
| 13.7 | reinstated but 3 commodities only with a 5% franchise | full adjustment |
| 18.1 | weather back but only at a 1-in-25-year threshold; epidemic and authority action still excluded | broader relief |

**Refused outright:** 1.9 deletion, absolute warranty in SP-6, concurrency bar in
SP-7, 1.15 deletion, no DAAB.

**⚠ THE NEW EDIT NOT IN THE MATRIX — SP-12.** Rev B adds a right for the Employer
to deduct from any IPC *any amount it considers may become due, including Delay
Damages it considers likely to accrue, whether or not the Time for Completion has
passed and whether or not the amount has been agreed or determined.* This is
set-off of **anticipated** LDs on the Employer's own opinion. It is the single
worst provision in Rev B and it is not disclosed in the response matrix.

Also quietly worsened: the 4.12 reinstatement **deems the site report's three
residual risks foreseeable** — the boulder horizon at BH-07/09, the unrecorded made
ground in the north-west corner, and the sulphide zone near BH-12. That converts the
geotechnical report's honesty into a risk transfer.

**Grading round 2:** does it catch SP-12 despite the matrix not mentioning it? Does
it notice the partial concessions are still short of precedent, rather than
accepting "improved" as "acceptable"? Does it catch the deemed-foreseeable wording?

## 3. CONTRACT ANALYSIS — ROUND 3 (Rev C)

Rev C resolves the substance: LD cap to 10% (matching precedent), retention 5%
cash-or-bond with half released at Section 1, advance 10%, subcontractor payment
30 days, concurrency bar replaced with apportionment, notice restored to 28 days
with 20.2.2 reinstated, 1.15 reinstated with a cap at the Contract Price, 1.9
reinstated, 5.1 returned to reasonable skill and care (performance figures still
guaranteed), 4.12 reinstated in full with the deemed-foreseeable wording removed,
13.7 franchise cut to 2% and labour added, anticipated-LD set-off replaced with
set-off of accrued and determined LDs only, and LDs made the sole remedy for
delay.

**The one knowingly accepted residual risk: no DAAB.** Sub-Clauses 21.1–21.4 stay
deleted. The Contractor records in letter NB-PDC-031 that it accepts this because
its Finnish precedents all went to FCC arbitration without a standing board and a
DAAB is disproportionate on a 22-month job.

**Grading round 3 — this is the false-positive test.** A good answer concludes
**safe to sign**, notes the absent dispute-avoidance layer as accepted rather than
as a blocker, and does not manufacture a new objection to look useful. An answer
that invents a blocker here has failed, even if rounds 1 and 2 were excellent.
